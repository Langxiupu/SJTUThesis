\begin{abstract}[zh]
IMT-2030 6G 愿景中，低轨（Low Earth Orbit，LEO）卫星网络已从补充覆盖系统扩展为非地面接入、星间回传与全球业务承载的空间基础设施。再生式载荷与星间链路使 LEO 星座的网络管控从地面集中配置转向星上自治决策。然而，大规模 LEO 星座面临拓扑高速变化、业务时空分布非均匀、星间回传容量受限和星上资源有限等约束。传统全网状态汇聚与集中式调度难以直接应用。6G 空天地一体化网络需要 LEO 星座实现分布式资源管控和用户级服务质量（Quality of Service，QoS）保障。

用户级 QoS 保障贯穿网络状态感知、用户接入与切换、无线资源调度和可靠传输四个环节，需星上节点在缺失可靠全局状态、地面实时控制闭环受限和星上通信与计算资源受限时，依托本地观测、有限邻域信令和在线决策协同管控。星上分布式管控面临四类关键问题：邻域视图不准确削弱分布式路由可信状态输入；高动态多重覆盖耦合目标卫星、随机接入资源和子信道竞争，弱服务用户难以维持持续可用接入；业务突发与信道波动使候选资源预算对长期用户级 QoS 支撑能力难以前瞻评估；星上算力与链路带宽受限使任务相关语义信息难以可靠恢复。本文据此构建面向端到端 QoS 保障的分布式协同资源管控体系。该体系沿大规模 LEO 星座的端到端业务承载路径，针对上述四个环节设计四项关键技术：多源置信度调控的分布式网络状态感知（CA-LDP）、基于局部公平性估计的分布式协作接入切换（FG-MAPPO）、基于生成式网络状态感知的切片--MAC 分层协作优化（DTPS）与基于任务反馈与协同增量重传的轻量化语义传输（CIS-R）。

（1）针对邻域视图不准确造成分布式路由状态输入不可靠，提出多源置信度调控的分布式网络状态感知（CA-LDP）框架。CA-LDP 将本地状态感知分为状态校准与置信度约束传播两阶段。校准阶段联合利用译码观测、时序先验、邻域空间支撑和信息新鲜度对不准确邻域状态进行置信度自适应图信号修复。传播阶段通过置信度加权聚合限制低可信状态的传播强度。在 1584 颗卫星的 Starlink 星座上，CA-LDP 在轻载和中载六个负载--BER 工作点相对各工作点最优基线的平均有效传输速率增益为 $7.47\%$，各工作点增益为 $6.63\%$--$8.82\%$。在全部九个工作点上，CA-LDP 相对各工作点最优基线的平均端到端时延降幅为 $4.75\%$，各工作点降幅为 $2.81\%$--$5.98\%$，业务传输成功率均为 $100\%$。

（2）针对高动态多重覆盖下目标卫星、随机接入资源与子信道竞争耦合，长期弱服务用户难以维持接入服务持续可用性，提出基于局部公平性估计的分布式协作接入切换（FG-MAPPO）方法。在线切换时，FG-MAPPO 将用户长期累计接入效用作为邻域信令，使终端低开销感知邻域长期服务状态。训练时采用广义 Gini 公平聚合函数构造公平性修正训练信号，通过吞吐优先--公平修正（TOFO）分立结构降低效率与公平性冲突目标的值估计方差。该机制兼容 3GPP 条件切换协议，仅用本地观测和有限邻域信令联合考虑候选卫星的链路质量、负载状态和长期公平性。仿真表明，仅交互邻域信息时，FG-MAPPO 相较具备全局子信道状态的集中式负载均衡切换策略，将广义 Gini 公平性指标提升 $3.48\%$，平均速率提升 $6.91\%$。相较于用户不协作的独立强化学习切换方法，FG-MAPPO 将切换成功率提升 $15.65\%$，星间负载方差降低 $30.91\%$，邻域信令开销仅 $7.73$ kB/s。

（3）针对业务突发、信道波动和用户竞争导致候选切片预算对长期用户级 QoS 支撑能力难以前瞻评估，提出基于生成式网络状态感知的切片--MAC 分层协作优化（DTPS）框架。切片层通过生成式网络状态感知学习候选预算下未来 QoS 演化关系，为 MAC 层提供 QoS 保障所需长期资源预算。MAC 层估计候选流满足速率--时延门限的最小资源开销，筛除不可满足流，执行 QCI 优先级加权选择。Starlink 星座仿真结果表明，在过载业务下，DTPS 的用户级 QoS 满足率为 $80.8\%$，TDRL-RSUA、RL-JRSS、LACO 与 DQN-MAC 四种基线为 $33.0\%$--$56.3\%$。与采用 PPO 切片资源分配和 Lyapunov MAC 调度的 TDRL-RSUA 组合基线相比，DTPS 将 QoS 满足率提高 $47.8$ 个百分点，平均时延为 $78.3$~ms。突发不确定性增强时，DTPS 仍将 QoS 满足率维持在 $97.6\%$ 以上。

（4）针对星上算力受限条件下轻量语义编码器难以通过单次传输保证地面任务性能的问题，提出基于任务反馈与协同增量重传的轻量化语义传输（CIS-R）机制。CIS-R 采用无需在星上发送端部署下游任务模型的收发架构，将任务模型推理与反馈计算部署在地面接收端，并根据当前任务判决对分块级接收表征的敏感度反馈后续传输优先级，以有限的增量通信开销换取任务性能。在高压缩率图像传输条件下，三轮增量传输将 ImageNet-100 的 Top-1 分类准确率提高 $12.24$ 个百分点。在遥感卫星过顶北京密云、喀什和三亚地面站的星地图像传输仿真中，在前向传输与反馈开销相同的条件下，任务驱动反馈较随机反馈将双任务平均分类准确率分别提高 $5.4$、$5.4$ 和 $4.5$ 个百分点。在当前实现的统计口径下，CIS-R 通信模型参数量仅为生成式 DiffJSCC 方案的约 $1/2400$，验证了以增量通信替代星上模型规模扩展的可行性。

综上，本文构建了面向端到端 QoS 保障的分布式协同资源管控体系。星座参数仿真与语义传输实验验证了该体系的有效性，为有限邻域信令和受限星上算力条件下的大规模 LEO 星座自治提供了可行路径。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract}[en]
Under the IMT-2030 6G vision, low Earth orbit (LEO) satellite networks have expanded from complementary coverage systems into space infrastructure for non-terrestrial access, inter-satellite backhaul, and global service delivery. Regenerative payloads and inter-satellite links enable network management in LEO constellations to shift from ground-centric centralized configuration to onboard autonomous decision-making. However, large-scale LEO constellations face constraints such as rapidly varying topology, nonuniform spatiotemporal traffic distribution, constrained inter-satellite backhaul capacity, and limited onboard resources. Conventional network-wide state aggregation and centralized scheduling are difficult to apply directly. 6G space-air-ground integrated networks require LEO constellations to achieve distributed resource management and user-level quality-of-service (QoS) assurance.

User-level QoS assurance spans four functions: network-state awareness, user access and handover, wireless resource scheduling, and reliable transmission. It requires onboard nodes to perform cooperative management through local observations, limited neighborhood signaling, and online decisions when reliable global states are unavailable, real-time ground control loops are constrained, and onboard communication and computing resources are limited. Onboard distributed management faces four key challenges: inaccurate neighborhood views undermine the trusted state input for distributed routing; highly dynamic multi-coverage couples competition over target satellites, random-access resources, and subchannels, making continuous access difficult to maintain for weak-service users; traffic bursts and channel fluctuations make it difficult to forecast whether candidate resource budgets can support long-term user-level QoS; and constrained onboard computing and link bandwidth make reliable recovery of task-relevant semantic information difficult. This dissertation develops a distributed cooperative resource-management framework for end-to-end QoS assurance. Along the end-to-end service-bearing path of large-scale LEO constellations, four techniques are designed for these functions: distributed network-state awareness with multi-source confidence regulation (CA-LDP), distributed cooperative access and handover based on local fairness estimation (FG-MAPPO), slice--MAC hierarchical cooperative optimization based on generative network-state awareness (DTPS), and lightweight semantic transmission based on task feedback and coordinated incremental retransmission (CIS-R).

(1) To address unreliable distributed-routing state input caused by inaccurate neighborhood views, this dissertation proposes a distributed network-state awareness framework with multi-source confidence regulation (CA-LDP). CA-LDP divides local state awareness into two stages: state calibration and confidence-constrained propagation. The calibration stage jointly exploits decoded observations, temporal priors, neighborhood spatial support, and information freshness to recover inaccurate neighborhood states through confidence-adaptive graph-signal restoration. The propagation stage uses confidence-weighted aggregation to limit the propagation strength of low-confidence states. On a 1584-satellite Starlink constellation, CA-LDP yields an average effective-transmission-rate gain of $7.47\%$ over the best baseline at each of six light- and medium-load operating points defined by traffic load and BER; the individual gains range from $6.63\%$ to $8.82\%$. Across all nine operating points, its average end-to-end delay reduction is $4.75\%$, with individual reductions from $2.81\%$ to $5.98\%$, and its traffic-delivery success ratio remains $100\%$.

(2) To address coupled competition for target satellites, random-access resources, and subchannels under highly dynamic multi-coverage, which makes continuous access-service availability difficult to maintain for long-term weak-service users, this dissertation proposes the FG-MAPPO method based on local fairness estimation. During online handover, FG-MAPPO uses each user's long-term cumulative access utility as neighborhood signaling, enabling terminals to sense the long-term service state of the neighborhood with low overhead. During training, this method employs a generalized Gini fairness aggregation function to construct fairness-corrected training signals and uses the throughput-oriented fairness-correction (TOFO) decoupled structure to reduce value-estimation variance for the conflicting objectives of efficiency and fairness. The mechanism is compatible with the 3GPP Conditional Handover protocol and jointly considers candidate-link quality, satellite load, and long-term fairness using only local observations and limited neighborhood signaling. Simulations show that with only neighborhood information exchange, compared with a centralized load-balancing handover strategy using global subchannel-state information, FG-MAPPO improves the generalized Gini fairness metric by $3.48\%$ and the average rate by $6.91\%$. Relative to a non-cooperative independent reinforcement-learning handover method, FG-MAPPO improves the handover success ratio by $15.65\%$ and reduces inter-satellite load variance by $30.91\%$, with neighborhood signaling overhead of only $7.73$ kB/s.

(3) To address the difficulty of forecasting whether candidate slice budgets can support long-term user-level QoS under traffic bursts, channel fluctuations, and user competition, this dissertation proposes the DTPS framework based on generative network-state awareness. At the slice layer, DTPS learns the future QoS evolution under candidate budgets through generative network-state awareness, thereby providing the MAC layer with long-term resource budgets required for QoS assurance. At the MAC layer, DTPS estimates the minimum resource cost for each candidate flow to satisfy its rate--delay thresholds, filters out unsatisfiable flows, and performs QCI-priority-weighted selection. Starlink constellation simulations show that under overloaded traffic, DTPS achieves a user-level QoS satisfaction ratio of $80.8\%$; the four baselines, TDRL-RSUA, RL-JRSS, LACO, and DQN-MAC, achieve $33.0\%$ to $56.3\%$. Compared with the TDRL-RSUA composite baseline, which uses PPO-based slicing and Lyapunov MAC scheduling, DTPS increases the satisfaction ratio by $47.8$ percentage points and yields an average delay of $78.3$~ms. The ratio remains above $97.6\%$ as burst uncertainty increases.

(4) To address the difficulty of maintaining ground-side task performance with a lightweight onboard semantic encoder and a single transmission, this dissertation proposes coordinated incremental semantic retransmission (CIS-R). CIS-R does not require a downstream task model to be deployed at the satellite transmitter; task inference and feedback computation are instead performed at the ground receiver. The receiver evaluates the sensitivity of the current task decision to patch-level received representations and feeds back the priorities for subsequent transmissions, thereby trading limited incremental communication overhead for task performance. Under high-compression image transmission, three incremental rounds improve the ImageNet-100 Top-1 classification accuracy by $12.24$ percentage points. In satellite--ground image transmission simulations covering remote-sensing satellite passes over the Beijing Miyun, Kashgar, and Sanya ground stations, task-driven feedback improves the average accuracy across two classification tasks by $5.4$, $5.4$, and $4.5$ percentage points, respectively, over random feedback with the same forward and feedback overhead. Under the current implementation-level accounting, the CIS-R communication model has approximately $1/2400$ of the parameters of the generative DiffJSCC scheme, demonstrating the feasibility of reducing onboard model complexity through incremental communication.

In summary, this dissertation builds a distributed cooperative resource-management framework for end-to-end QoS assurance in large-scale LEO constellations. Constellation-parameter simulations and semantic-transmission experiments verify its effectiveness, providing a feasible path toward onboard autonomy under limited neighborhood signaling and constrained onboard computing resources.
\end{abstract}
