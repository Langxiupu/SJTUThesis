\begin{abstract}[zh]
IMT-2030 6G 愿景中，低轨（Low Earth Orbit，LEO）卫星网络已从补充覆盖系统扩展为非地面接入、星间回传与全球业务承载的空间基础设施。再生式载荷与星间链路使 LEO 星座的网络管控从地面集中配置转向星上自治决策。然而，大规模 LEO 星座面临拓扑高速变化、业务时空分布非均匀、星间回传容量受限和星上资源有限等约束。传统全网状态汇聚与集中式调度难以直接应用。6G 空天地一体化网络需要 LEO 星座实现分布式资源管控和用户级服务质量（Quality of Service，QoS）保障。

用户级 QoS 保障贯穿网络状态感知、接入与切换、无线资源调度和可靠传输，需星上节点在缺失可靠全局状态、地面实时控制闭环受限和星上通信与计算资源受限时，依托本地观测、有限邻域信令和在线决策协同管控。星上分布式管控面临四类关键问题：邻域视图不准确削弱分布式路由可信状态输入；高动态多重覆盖耦合目标卫星、随机接入资源和子信道竞争，弱服务用户难以维持持续可用接入；业务突发与信道波动使候选资源预算对长期用户级 QoS 支撑能力难以前瞻评估；星上算力与链路带宽受限使任务相关语义信息难以可靠恢复。提出大规模 LEO 星座分布式协同资源管控方法与架构，沿端到端业务承载路径设计四项关键技术：多源置信度调控的分布式网络状态感知、基于局部公平性估计的分布式协作接入切换、基于生成式网络状态感知的切片--MAC 分层协作优化与基于任务反馈与协同增量重传的轻量化语义传输。

（1）针对邻域视图不准确造成分布式路由状态输入不可靠，提出多源置信度调控的分布式网络状态感知框架（CA-LDP）。CA-LDP 将本地状态感知分为状态校准与置信度约束传播两阶段。校准阶段联合利用译码观测、时序先验、邻域空间支撑和信息新鲜度对不准确邻域状态进行置信度自适应图信号修复。传播阶段通过置信度加权聚合限制低可信状态的传播强度。在 1584 颗卫星的 Starlink 星座和九类受控邻域视图不准确场景下，CA-LDP 相比基线方法将平均传输速率提升 $4.8\%$--$10.8\%$，端到端时延降低 $6.7\%$--$11.1\%$，路由成功率接近 $100\%$。

（2）针对高动态多重覆盖下目标卫星、随机接入资源与子信道竞争耦合，长期弱服务用户难以维持接入服务持续可用性，提出基于局部公平性估计的 FG-MAPPO 方法。在线切换时，FG-MAPPO 将用户长期累计接入效用作为邻域信令，使终端低开销感知邻域长期服务状态。训练时采用广义 Gini 公平聚合函数构造公平性修正训练信号，通过吞吐优先--公平修正（TOFO）分立结构降低效率与公平性冲突目标的值估计方差。该机制兼容 3GPP 条件切换（CHO）流程，仅用本地观测和有限邻域信令联合考虑候选卫星的链路质量、负载状态和长期公平性。仿真表明，仅交互邻域信息时，FG-MAPPO 的广义 Gini 公平性指标较集中式规划提升 $3.48\%$，平均速率提升 $6.91\%$。较分布式强化学习基线，切换成功率与负载均衡性能最高分别提升 $15.65\%$ 与 $30.91\%$，邻域信令开销仅 $7.73$ kB/s。

（3）针对业务突发、信道波动和用户竞争导致候选切片预算对长期用户级 QoS 支撑能力难以前瞻评估，提出基于生成式网络状态感知的 DTPS 框架。切片层通过生成式网络状态感知学习候选预算下未来 QoS 演化关系，为 MAC 层提供 QoS 保障所需长期资源预算。MAC 层估计候选流满足速率--时延门限的最小资源开销，筛除不可满足流，执行 QCI 优先级加权选择。Starlink 规模星座仿真中，DTPS 在过载业务下实现 $80.8\%$ 的用户级 QoS 满足率，而 TDRL-RSUA、RL-JRSS、LACO 与 DQN-MAC 等基线方法的 QoS 满足率为 $33.0\%$--$56.3\%$。DTPS 平均时延保持在 $78.3$ ms。突发不确定性增强时，QoS 满足率维持在 $97.6\%$ 以上。

（4）针对星上算力与链路带宽受限条件下任务相关语义信息难以可靠恢复，提出基于任务反馈与协同增量重传的 CIS-HARQ 机制。该机制将可靠性增益从模型复杂度扩展转向通信轮次与反馈协作，将完整重传重构为任务反馈引导的选择性增量编码与跨轮合并。接收端根据当前语义状态与任务特征差距生成反馈，发射端据此生成选择性语义增量，接收端逐轮恢复任务相关特征。在 ImageNet-100、CIFAR-100 与 VeRi-776 数据集、Rician 衰落信道、信噪比 $5$ dB 且信道带宽比（CBR）约 $0.02$ 的紧带宽条件下，CIS-HARQ 经三轮增量传输将下游任务精度由同带宽基线的 $35.7\%$ 提升至 $65.0\%$。该机制参数量约 $2.2$ M，星上推理时延毫秒级，较生成式扩散接收端参数量减少约 $2500$ 倍，任务精度差距控制在约 $7\%$ 以内。

本文沿网络状态感知、用户接入与切换、无线资源调度和可靠传输四个环节构建分布式协同管控体系，基于真实星座参数仿真与语义传输实验，验证所提方法对可信状态感知、接入服务持续可用、长期用户级 QoS 保障及受限链路下语义传输可靠性的改善。本文将大规模 LEO 星座资源管控从分层独立优化推进至端到端 QoS 导向的分布式协同管控，在有限邻域信令与可控星上算力约束下，为空天地一体化网络的星上自治提供可行技术路径。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract}[en]
Under the IMT-2030 6G vision, low Earth orbit (LEO) satellite networks have expanded from complementary coverage systems into space infrastructure for non-terrestrial access, inter-satellite backhaul, and global service delivery. Regenerative payloads and inter-satellite links enable network management in LEO constellations to shift from ground-centric centralized configuration to onboard autonomous decision-making. However, large-scale LEO constellations face constraints such as rapidly varying topology, nonuniform spatiotemporal traffic distribution, constrained inter-satellite backhaul capacity, and limited onboard resources. Conventional network-wide state aggregation and centralized scheduling are difficult to apply directly. 6G space-air-ground integrated networks require LEO constellations to achieve distributed resource management and user-level quality-of-service (QoS) assurance.

User-level QoS assurance spans network-state awareness, access and handover, wireless resource scheduling, and reliable transmission. It requires onboard nodes to perform cooperative management through local observations, limited neighborhood signaling, and online decisions when reliable global states are unavailable, real-time ground control loops are constrained, and onboard communication and computing resources are limited. Onboard distributed management faces four key challenges: inaccurate neighborhood views undermine the trusted state input for distributed routing; highly dynamic multi-coverage couples competition over target satellites, random-access resources, and subchannels, making continuous access difficult to maintain for weak-service users; traffic bursts and channel fluctuations make it difficult to forecast whether candidate resource budgets can support long-term user-level QoS; and constrained onboard computing and link bandwidth make reliable recovery of task-relevant semantic information difficult. A distributed cooperative resource-management method and architecture for large-scale LEO constellations are proposed, and four key techniques are developed along the end-to-end service-bearing path: distributed network-state awareness with multi-source confidence regulation, distributed cooperative access-handover based on local fairness estimation, slice--MAC hierarchical cooperative optimization based on generative network-state awareness, and lightweight semantic transmission based on task feedback and coordinated incremental retransmission.

(1) To address unreliable distributed-routing state input caused by inaccurate neighborhood views, this dissertation proposes a distributed network-state awareness framework with multi-source confidence regulation (CA-LDP). CA-LDP divides local state awareness into two stages: state calibration and confidence-constrained propagation. The calibration stage jointly exploits decoded observations, temporal priors, neighborhood spatial support, and information freshness to recover inaccurate neighborhood states through confidence-adaptive graph-signal restoration. The propagation stage uses confidence-weighted aggregation to limit the propagation strength of low-confidence states. In a 1584-satellite Starlink constellation and nine controlled inaccurate-neighborhood-view scenarios, CA-LDP improves the average transmission rate by $4.8\%$--$10.8\%$ and reduces end-to-end delay by $6.7\%$--$11.1\%$ compared with baseline methods, with routing success ratio close to $100\%$.

(2) To address coupled competition for target satellites, random-access resources, and subchannels under highly dynamic multi-coverage, which makes continuous access-service availability difficult to maintain for long-term weak-service users, this dissertation proposes the FG-MAPPO method based on local fairness estimation. During online handover, FG-MAPPO uses each user's long-term cumulative access utility as neighborhood signaling, enabling terminals to sense the long-term service state of the neighborhood with low overhead. During training, this method employs a generalized Gini fairness aggregation function to construct fairness-corrected training signals and uses the throughput-oriented fairness-correction (TOFO) decoupled structure to reduce value-estimation variance for the conflicting objectives of efficiency and fairness. The mechanism is compatible with the 3GPP Conditional Handover (CHO) process and jointly considers candidate-link quality, satellite load, and long-term fairness using only local observations and limited neighborhood signaling. Simulations show that with only neighborhood information exchange, FG-MAPPO improves the generalized Gini fairness metric by $3.48\%$ and the average rate by $6.91\%$ compared with centralized planning. Relative to distributed reinforcement-learning baselines, it improves the handover success ratio and load-balancing performance by up to $15.65\%$ and $30.91\%$, respectively, with neighborhood signaling overhead of only $7.73$ kB/s.

(3) To address the difficulty of forecasting whether candidate slice budgets can support long-term user-level QoS under traffic bursts, channel fluctuations, and user competition, this dissertation proposes the DTPS framework based on generative network-state awareness. At the slice layer, DTPS learns the future QoS evolution under candidate budgets through generative network-state awareness, thereby providing the MAC layer with long-term resource budgets required for QoS assurance. At the MAC layer, DTPS estimates the minimum resource cost for each candidate flow to satisfy its rate--delay thresholds, filters out unsatisfiable flows, and performs QCI-priority-weighted selection. In Starlink-scale constellation simulations, DTPS achieves a user-level QoS satisfaction ratio of $80.8\%$ under overloaded traffic, while baseline methods such as TDRL-RSUA, RL-JRSS, LACO, and DQN-MAC achieve QoS satisfaction ratios of $33.0\%$--$56.3\%$. DTPS keeps the average delay at $78.3$ ms. When burst uncertainty increases, the QoS satisfaction ratio remains above $97.6\%$.

(4) To address reliable recovery of task-relevant semantic information under constrained onboard computing and link bandwidth, this dissertation proposes the CIS-HARQ mechanism based on task feedback and coordinated incremental retransmission. This mechanism shifts reliability gain from model-complexity expansion to communication rounds and feedback cooperation, reformulating full retransmission as task-feedback-guided selective incremental encoding and cross-round combining. The receiver generates feedback from the gap between the current semantic state and task features, the transmitter accordingly generates selective semantic increments, and the receiver progressively recovers task-relevant features. On the ImageNet-100, CIFAR-100, and VeRi-776 datasets over a Rician fading channel, with an SNR of $5$ dB and a channel bandwidth ratio (CBR) of about $0.02$ under tight-bandwidth conditions, CIS-HARQ improves downstream task accuracy from $35.7\%$ for the equal-bandwidth baseline to $65.0\%$ after three incremental transmission rounds. The mechanism has about $2.2$ M parameters and millisecond-level onboard inference latency; compared with a generative diffusion receiver, it reduces the parameter count by about $2500$ times while keeping the task-accuracy gap within about $7\%$.

The proposed methods build a distributed cooperative management framework along network-state awareness, user access and handover, wireless resource scheduling, and reliable transmission. Based on real constellation-parameter simulations and semantic-transmission experiments, the results verify the improvements achieved by the proposed methods in trusted state awareness, continuous access-service availability, long-term user-level QoS assurance, and semantic-transmission reliability over constrained links. This dissertation advances resource management for large-scale LEO constellations from isolated layer-wise optimization to end-to-end QoS-oriented distributed cooperative management, and provides a feasible technical path toward onboard autonomy in 6G space-air-ground integrated networks under limited neighborhood signaling and controllable onboard computing constraints.
\end{abstract}
