\begin{abstract}[zh]
IMT-2030 6G 愿景下，低轨（Low Earth Orbit，LEO）卫星网络已从补充覆盖系统扩展为非地面接入、星间回传与全球业务承载的空间基础设施。再生式载荷与星间链路使 LEO 星座从地面集中管控转向星上自治决策。大规模 LEO 星座面临拓扑高速变化、业务时空分布不均、星间回传容量受限和星上资源有限等约束，传统全网状态汇聚与集中式调度难以适用。6G 空天地一体化网络要求 LEO 星座通过分布式协同资源管控保障用户级服务质量（Quality of Service，QoS）。

用户级 QoS 保障涵盖网络状态感知、用户接入与切换、无线资源调度和任务信息可靠恢复四个环节。当可靠全局状态缺失、地面实时控制受限且星上通信与计算资源受限时，星上节点需依托本地观测、有限邻域信令和在线决策实现协同管控。星上分布式管控面临四类问题：邻域视图不准确削弱分布式路由的可信状态输入；高动态多重覆盖下目标卫星、随机接入资源和子信道竞争相互耦合，弱服务用户难以维持持续接入；业务突发与信道波动使候选资源预算对长期用户级 QoS 的支撑能力难以前瞻评估；星上算力与链路带宽受限使任务相关语义信息难以可靠恢复。本文构建了面向端到端 QoS 保障的分布式协同资源管控体系，针对大规模 LEO 星座端到端业务承载路径设计四项关键技术：多源置信度调控的分布式网络状态感知（CA-LDP）、基于局部公平性估计的分布式协作接入切换（FG-MAPPO）、基于生成式网络状态感知的切片--MAC 分层协作优化（DTPS）与基于任务反馈与协同增量重传的任务信息可靠恢复（CIS-R）。

（1）针对邻域视图不准确导致分布式路由状态输入不可靠，提出多源置信度调控的分布式网络状态感知（CA-LDP）框架。CA-LDP 将本地状态感知分为状态校准与置信度约束传播两阶段。校准阶段联合利用译码观测、时序先验、邻域空间支撑和信息新鲜度，通过置信度自适应图信号修复不准确邻域状态。传播阶段通过置信度加权聚合抑制低置信度状态扩散。在 1584 颗卫星的 Starlink 星座仿真中，针对由轻载、中载与三档 BER 组合形成的六个工作点，CA-LDP 相对最优基线的平均有效传输速率增益为 $7.47\%$，单点增益为 $6.63\%$--$8.82\%$。在全部九个工作点上，CA-LDP 相对最优基线的平均端到端时延降幅为 $4.75\%$，各工作点降幅为 $2.81\%$--$5.98\%$，业务传输成功率均为 $100\%$。

（2）针对高动态多重覆盖下目标卫星、随机接入资源与子信道竞争耦合导致弱服务用户难以维持接入连续性，提出基于局部公平性估计的分布式协作接入切换（FG-MAPPO）机制。在线切换时，FG-MAPPO 以用户长期累计接入效用作为邻域信令，使终端以低开销感知邻域长期服务状态。训练时采用广义 Gini 公平聚合函数构造公平性修正训练信号，提高低接入效用排序权重；吞吐优先--公平修正（TOFO）分立结构降低效率与公平性冲突目标的值估计方差。该机制兼容 3GPP 条件切换协议，仅用本地观测和有限邻域信令即可兼顾候选卫星链路质量、负载状态和长期公平性。仿真仅交互邻域信息时，FG-MAPPO 相较具备全局子信道状态的集中式负载均衡切换策略，尾部加权广义 Gini 公平性指标提升 $3.48\%$，平均速率提升 $6.91\%$。相较用户不协作的独立强化学习切换方法，FG-MAPPO 将切换成功率提升 $15.65\%$，星间负载方差降低 $30.91\%$；全互联条件下在线协作信令开销上界为 $7.73$ KiB/s。

（3）针对业务突发、信道波动和用户竞争导致候选切片预算对长期用户级 QoS 支撑能力难以前瞻评估，提出基于生成式网络状态感知的切片--MAC 分层协作优化（DTPS）框架。切片层通过生成式网络状态感知学习候选预算下的未来 QoS 演化，为 MAC 层提供 QoS 保障所需的长期资源预算。MAC 层估计候选流达到综合 QoS 效用门限的最小资源开销，筛除无法达标的流，按 QCI 优先级加权选择。Starlink 星座仿真表明，过载业务下，DTPS 用户级 QoS 满足率为 $80.8\%$，而 TDRL-RSUA、RL-JRSS、LACO 与 DQN-MAC 四种基线为 $33.0\%$--$56.3\%$。相较采用 PPO 切片资源分配和 Lyapunov MAC 调度的 TDRL-RSUA 基线，DTPS 将 QoS 满足率提高 $47.8$ 个百分点，平均时延为 $78.3$~ms。突发不确定性增强时，其 QoS 满足率仍保持在 $97.6\%$ 以上。

（4）针对星上算力受限时轻量语义编码器单次传输难以保证地面任务性能，提出基于任务反馈与协同增量重传的任务信息可靠恢复（CIS-R）机制。CIS-R 无需在星上发送端部署下游任务模型，将任务推理与反馈计算置于地面接收端，根据当前任务判决对分块级接收表征的敏感度反馈后续传输优先级，以有限增量通信开销换取任务性能提升。在高压缩率图像传输条件下，三轮增量传输将 ImageNet-100 的 Top-1 分类准确率提高 $12.24$ 个百分点。在遥感卫星过顶北京密云、喀什和三亚地面站的星地图像传输仿真中，前向传输与反馈开销相同时，任务驱动反馈较随机反馈将双任务平均分类准确率分别提高 $5.4$、$5.4$ 和 $4.5$ 个百分点。CIS-R 通信模型参数量仅为生成式 DiffJSCC 方案的约 $1/2500$，验证了以增量通信替代星上模型规模扩展的可行性。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract}[en]
Under the IMT-2030 6G vision, low Earth orbit (LEO) satellite networks have evolved from complementary coverage systems into space infrastructure for non-terrestrial access, inter-satellite backhaul, and global service delivery. Regenerative payloads and inter-satellite links shift LEO constellation management from centralized ground control to autonomous onboard decision-making. Large-scale LEO constellation management is challenged by rapidly varying network topology, uneven spatiotemporal traffic distribution, limited inter-satellite backhaul capacity, and constrained onboard resources. These factors make conventional network-wide state aggregation and centralized scheduling impractical. 6G space-air-ground integrated networks require distributed cooperative resource management in LEO constellations to assure user-level quality of service (QoS).

User-level QoS assurance covers four functions: network-state awareness, user access and handover, wireless resource scheduling, and reliable recovery of task information. When reliable global states are unavailable, ground-based real-time control is limited, and onboard communication and computing resources are constrained, onboard nodes must coordinate resource management through local observations, limited neighborhood signaling, and online decisions. Four problems arise: inaccurate neighborhood views weaken the reliable state input for distributed routing; under highly dynamic multi-coverage, contention for target satellites, random-access resources, and subchannels becomes mutually coupled, making continuous access difficult for weak-service users; traffic bursts and channel fluctuations hinder assessment of whether candidate resource budgets can support long-term user-level QoS; and limited onboard computing and link bandwidth impede reliable recovery of task-relevant semantic information. This dissertation develops a distributed cooperative resource-management framework for end-to-end QoS assurance. For the end-to-end service path of a large-scale LEO constellation, the framework presents four key techniques: distributed network-state awareness with multi-source confidence regulation (CA-LDP), distributed cooperative access and handover based on local fairness estimation (FG-MAPPO), slice--MAC hierarchical cooperative optimization based on generative network-state awareness (DTPS), and reliable recovery of task information based on task feedback and coordinated incremental retransmission (CIS-R).

(1) To address unreliable distributed-routing state input caused by inaccurate neighborhood views, this dissertation proposes a distributed network-state awareness framework with multi-source confidence regulation (CA-LDP). CA-LDP divides local state awareness into state calibration and confidence-constrained propagation. During calibration, decoded observations, temporal priors, neighborhood spatial support, and information freshness jointly enable confidence-adaptive graph-signal restoration of inaccurate neighborhood states. During propagation, confidence-weighted aggregation suppresses dissemination of low-confidence states. In Starlink constellation simulations with 1584 satellites, CA-LDP achieves an average effective transmission rate gain of $7.47\%$ over the best baseline at six light- and medium-load operating points defined by traffic load and BER, with individual gains of $6.63\%$ to $8.82\%$. Across all nine operating points, CA-LDP reduces the average end-to-end delay by $4.75\%$ over the best baseline, with individual reductions of $2.81\%$ to $5.98\%$, while maintaining a $100\%$ traffic delivery success ratio.

(2) To address the coupled contention for target satellites, random-access resources, and subchannels under highly dynamic multi-coverage, which prevents weak-service users from maintaining continuous access, this dissertation proposes distributed cooperative access and handover based on local fairness estimation (FG-MAPPO). During online handover, FG-MAPPO uses each user's long-term cumulative access utility as neighborhood signaling, allowing terminals to sense long-term neighborhood service conditions with low overhead. During training, a generalized Gini fairness aggregation function constructs a fairness-corrected training signal and assigns larger weights to lower-ranked access utilities. The separate throughput-oriented and fairness-oriented (TOFO) structure reduces value-estimation variance between the conflicting efficiency and fairness objectives. The mechanism is compatible with the 3GPP Conditional Handover protocol and accounts for candidate-satellite link quality, load, and long-term fairness using only local observations and limited neighborhood signaling. With only neighborhood information exchanged, simulations show that FG-MAPPO improves the tail-weighted generalized Gini fairness metric by $3.48\%$ and the average rate by $6.91\%$ relative to centralized load-balancing handover with global subchannel-state information. Relative to noncooperative independent reinforcement-learning handover, FG-MAPPO improves the handover success ratio by $15.65\%$ and reduces inter-satellite load variance by $30.91\%$. Under all-to-all exchange, the upper bound on online cooperative signaling overhead is $7.73$ KiB/s.

(3) To address the difficulty of assessing whether candidate slice budgets can support long-term user-level QoS under traffic bursts, channel fluctuations, and user contention, this dissertation proposes slice--MAC hierarchical cooperative optimization based on generative network-state awareness (DTPS). The slice layer uses generative network-state awareness to learn future QoS evolution under candidate budgets and provides the MAC layer with the long-term resource budgets required for QoS assurance. The MAC layer estimates the minimum resource cost for each candidate flow to reach the composite QoS utility threshold, removes flows that cannot meet the threshold, and performs QCI-priority-weighted selection. Starlink constellation simulation results show that under overloaded traffic, DTPS achieves a user-level QoS satisfaction ratio of $80.8\%$, whereas the four baselines TDRL-RSUA, RL-JRSS, LACO, and DQN-MAC achieve $33.0\%$ to $56.3\%$. Relative to the TDRL-RSUA baseline using PPO-based slice resource allocation and Lyapunov MAC scheduling, DTPS increases the QoS satisfaction ratio by $47.8$ percentage points and yields an average delay of $78.3$~ms. Its QoS satisfaction ratio remains above $97.6\%$ as burst uncertainty increases.

(4) To address the difficulty of guaranteeing ground-side task performance when a lightweight onboard semantic encoder transmits only once, this dissertation proposes reliable recovery of task information based on task feedback and coordinated incremental retransmission (CIS-R). CIS-R does not deploy the downstream task model at the satellite transmitter. Instead, the ground receiver performs task-model inference and feedback computation. Based on the sensitivity of the current task decision to block-level received representations, the receiver feeds back subsequent transmission priorities, trading limited incremental communication overhead for improved task performance. Under high-compression image transmission, three incremental rounds improve ImageNet-100 Top-1 classification accuracy by $12.24$ percentage points. In satellite--ground image transmission simulations for remote-sensing satellite passes over the Beijing Miyun, Kashgar, and Sanya ground stations, with identical forward-transmission and feedback overhead, task-driven feedback improves the mean accuracy across two classification tasks by $5.4$, $5.4$, and $4.5$ percentage points, respectively, over random feedback. The CIS-R communication model contains approximately $1/2500$ as many parameters as the generative DiffJSCC scheme, confirming the feasibility of replacing onboard model scaling with incremental communication.
\end{abstract}
