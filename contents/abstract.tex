\begin{abstract}[zh]
在 IMT-2030 定义的第六代移动通信技术（6G）愿景中，泛在连接与万物智联被确立为下一代信息基础设施的核心目标。低轨（Low Earth Orbit，LEO）卫星网络凭借其广覆盖、低时延与高容量特性，被视为 6G 体系中实现非地面接入与回传、拓展网络空间维度的关键基础设施。随着低成本、小型化卫星载荷平台的持续演进以及人工智能（Artificial Intelligence，AI）驱动的网络管控技术不断成熟，一方面，星上算力的提升使 LEO 星座逐步具备在轨智能处理与协同决策能力；另一方面，AI 正由地面离线辅助优化向星上在线决策演进，推动卫星网络管控体系由集中式调度向具备“自感知—自决策—自优化”特征的边缘自治模式发展。AI 驱动的 LEO 卫星管控为面向用户 QoS 的端到端资源编排提供了新的实现路径，并支撑 LEO 卫星系统向 AI 原生、算网融合的信息网络演进。

LEO 卫星网络在高速运动、不可靠星地/星间链以及星上资源受限等因素共同作用下，具有QoS保障的端到端传输面临显著挑战。为了实现LEO卫星网络均有业务QoS保障的端到端传输，本文从用户移动性管理、接入网资源分配以及端到端传输三个角度开展研究，重点围绕集中–分布式协同的智能管控架构、面向公平性优化的分布式用户协作切换机制、面向应用级 QoS 的跨层资源编排与无线资源分配机制，以及任务感知的语义 HARQ 与路由协同机制等关键技术展开系统设计与分析。本文旨在网络管控范式、AI 驱动的管控方法论构建以及端到端传输性能优化三个层面，系统性提升LEO卫星的资源管控能力。在管控架构层面，本文通过将部分核心网功能与决策能力下沉至卫星网络边缘侧，以解决不可靠的回程链路所带来的传输开销与实时性的问题。在 AI 驱动的管控方法论层面，本文基于3GPP定义的LEO卫星协议栈，研究多智能体强化学习在资源管控中的应用机制，构建可轻量化部署的、具有理论可解释性的智能管控方法。在性能优化层面，本文引入用户协作与通信机制，并在接入侧与路由侧分别设计跨层调度机制，以提升服务连续性、QoS保障能力以及端到端传输的可靠性。本文主要的工作与创新点如下：

（1）在用户协作切换机制方面，针对LEO卫星随机接入体制下缺乏全局协调机制、海量用户下信道冲突严重的问题，本文将用户接入公平性保障建模为一个最大化最小用户吞吐量的多目标优化问题，并基于多智能体近端策略优化算法（Multi-Agent Proximal Policy Optimization，MAPPO）在动态用户分布下实现公平性的长期优化。本文首先将多目标优化问题建模为多目标马尔可夫决策过程，并基于广义Gini社会福利函数对公平性与效率进行编码。基于上述优化目标，本文推导了多目标马尔科夫过程的策略梯度解析式，并证明用户可通过广播经Gini函数修正的优势值，在分布式协作架构下实现对邻域公平性的有效估计，且保持低通信开销。其次，为了提升多目标的训练收敛性，本文设计了混合神经网络结构来降低多目标价值估计的方差。最后，为支撑LEO卫星广域覆盖下大规模用户的协作切换学习，本文设计并实现了异步的分布式训练框架来提升训练的可扩展性。

（2）在跨层资源编排与无线资源分配机制方面，针对应用级 QoS 保障引发的决策空间维度爆炸，以及在非平稳、部分可观测条件下跨层调度难以自适应业务负载与信道以及业务队列积压变化的问题，本文提出了一种快–慢时间尺度的双层资源分配框架。该框架在切片层于慢时间尺度上联合进行带宽与功率份额分配，在切片内部于快时间尺度上实现功率与时频资源块到应用的映射，最终在满足应用级 QoS 约束下提升频谱利用率。本文首先将跨层资源分配问题建模为在应用级 QoS 约束与业务突发条件下最小化资源分配开销的混合整数随机优化问题。在切片层，设计显式学习环境动力学的世界模型，用于在部分可观测条件下预测单位资源块的服务能力，并据此生成具备前瞻性的带宽与功率分配决策。在 MAC 层，本文基于 Lyapunov 优化构建感知队列积压、子载波信噪比与世界模型预测的无线资源分配机制，在最大化加权服务收益的同时引入资源使用惩罚项，在应用级QoS保障下降低资源开销。

（3）在端到端可靠传输方面，针对 LEO 星座不可靠回传下传统 HARQ 频繁触发重传并显著引起端到端时延恶化与抖动的问题，本文提出一种任务感知的语义 HARQ 与语义驱动路由的协同优化机制。不同于依赖黑盒语义失真检测的现有方法，本文以下游任务输出置信度和互信息累积为判据设计重传触发门限，以降低重传次数。为实现语义驱动的选择性重传，本文引入积分梯度方法以评估语义特征块对下游任务的贡献度进，并选择重要的 top-k 语义特征块进行选择性重传。在此基础上，本文在接收端设计软合并解码机制，将历史接收语义与回传增量语义进行融合，实现语义表征的迭代优化。为了实现星间的可靠传输，在星间路由层面，本文利用语义层输出的语义价值排序对业务流进行区分，并为高语义价值数据按需分配拥塞避免的传输路径。

\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract}[en]
In the vision of sixth-generation mobile communication systems (6G) defined by IMT-2030, ubiquitous connectivity and intelligent interconnection of everything are identified as core objectives of next-generation information infrastructure. Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite networks, owing to their wide coverage, low latency, and high capacity, are regarded as a key infrastructure for enabling non-terrestrial access and backhaul and for extending the spatial dimension of 6G networks. With the continuous evolution of low-cost and miniaturized satellite payload platforms and the increasing maturity of artificial intelligence (AI)–driven network control technologies, on the one hand, the enhancement of onboard computing capabilities enables LEO constellations to gradually support in-orbit intelligent processing and cooperative decision-making; on the other hand, AI is evolving from ground-based offline auxiliary optimization toward onboard online decision-making, driving the satellite network control paradigm from centralized scheduling toward an edge-autonomous mode characterized by self-perception, self-decision, and self-optimization. AI-driven LEO satellite control provides a new realization path for end-to-end, QoS-oriented resource orchestration and supports the evolution of LEO satellite systems toward AI-native, computing–communication integrated information networks.

End-to-end transmission with QoS guarantees in LEO satellite networks faces significant challenges due to the combined effects of high-speed satellite mobility, unreliable satellite–ground and inter-satellite links, and limited onboard resources. To achieve end-to-end transmission with QoS guarantees for all services in LEO satellite networks, this dissertation conducts research from three perspectives, namely user mobility management, radio access network resource allocation, and end-to-end transmission, with a particular focus on the systematic design and analysis of key technologies including a centralized–distributed collaborative intelligent network control architecture, fairness-oriented distributed cooperative user handover mechanisms, application-level QoS-driven cross-layer resource orchestration and wireless resource allocation mechanisms, as well as task-aware semantic HARQ and routing coordination mechanisms. This dissertation aims to systematically enhance the resource management capability of LEO satellite networks at three levels: network control paradigms, the construction of AI-driven control methodologies, and end-to-end transmission performance optimization. At the network architecture level, this work pushes selected core network functions and decision-making capabilities toward the edge of the satellite network to address the transmission overhead and real-time limitations caused by unreliable backhaul links. At the AI-driven control methodology level, based on the LEO satellite protocol stack defined by 3GPP, this dissertation investigates the application mechanisms of multi-agent reinforcement learning in resource management and develops intelligent control methods that are lightweight for deployment and endowed with theoretical interpretability. At the performance optimization level, this work introduces user cooperation and communication mechanisms, and designs cross-layer scheduling mechanisms at both the access and routing layers to enhance service continuity, QoS assurance, and the reliability of end-to-end transmission. The main contributions and innovations of this dissertation are summarized as follows:

(1) Specifically, in terms of cooperative user handover, to address the lack of global coordination and severe channel contention under massive user access in the random access regime of LEO satellite networks, user access fairness is formulated as a multi-objective optimization problem that maximizes the minimum user throughput, and a multi-agent proximal policy optimization (MAPPO)–based approach is developed to achieve long-term fairness optimization under dynamic user distributions. The multi-objective optimization problem is modeled as a multi-objective Markov decision process, in which fairness and efficiency are encoded using a generalized Gini social welfare function. Based on the resulting optimization objective, the analytical form of the policy gradient for the multi-objective Markov process is derived, and it is shown that users can effectively estimate neighborhood-level fairness in a distributed cooperative architecture by broadcasting Gini-adjusted advantage values while maintaining low communication overhead. To further improve training convergence for multi-objective learning, a hybrid neural network architecture is designed to reduce the variance of multi-objective value estimation. In addition, to support large-scale cooperative handover learning under the wide-area coverage of LEO satellite networks, an asynchronous distributed training framework is designed and implemented to enhance training scalability.

(2) In terms of cross-layer resource orchestration and wireless resource allocation, to address the explosion of decision space induced by application-level QoS guarantees, as well as the difficulty of adapting cross-layer scheduling to dynamic traffic loads, channel conditions, and queue backlogs under non-stationary and partially observable environments, a dual-layer resource allocation framework with fast–slow time scales is proposed. In this framework, bandwidth and power shares are jointly allocated at the slice level on a slow time scale, while the mapping of power and time–frequency resource blocks to applications is performed within each slice on a fast time scale, thereby improving spectrum efficiency under application-level QoS constraints. The cross-layer resource allocation problem is formulated as a mixed-integer stochastic optimization problem that minimizes resource allocation cost under application-level QoS constraints and bursty traffic conditions. At the slice level, a world model that explicitly learns environment dynamics is designed to predict the service capability of individual resource blocks under partial observability, enabling foresighted bandwidth and power allocation decisions. At the MAC layer, a Lyapunov optimization-based wireless resource allocation mechanism is developed, which jointly considers queue backlog, subcarrier signal-to-noise ratio, and world-model predictions, and reduces resource consumption while ensuring application-level QoS by introducing resource usage penalty terms.

(3) In terms of end-to-end reliable transmission, to mitigate the severe end-to-end latency degradation and jitter caused by frequent retransmissions triggered by conventional HARQ under unreliable LEO backhaul links, a task-aware semantic HARQ scheme jointly optimized with semantic-driven routing is proposed. Unlike existing approaches that rely on black-box semantic distortion detection, retransmission triggering thresholds are designed based on downstream task output confidence and accumulated mutual information, thereby reducing retransmission frequency. To enable semantic-driven selective retransmission, the integrated gradients method is employed to evaluate the contribution of semantic feature blocks to downstream tasks, and the most important top-k semantic feature blocks are selected for retransmission. Furthermore, a soft-combining decoding mechanism is designed at the receiver to fuse historically received semantics with incremental retransmitted semantics, enabling iterative optimization of semantic representations. To enhance reliability over inter-satellite links, semantic value-based traffic differentiation is applied at the routing layer, where congestion-avoidance transmission paths are dynamically allocated to data with high semantic value.
\end{abstract}
