\begin{abstract}[zh]
IMT-2030 6G 愿景下，再生式载荷与星间链路推动低轨（Low Earth Orbit，LEO）卫星网络由补充覆盖系统向非地面接入、星间回传与全球业务承载的空间基础设施演进，并使其管控方式由地面集中管控转向星上自治决策。面对大规模 LEO 星座拓扑高速变化、业务时空分布不均、星间回传容量和星上资源受限等约束，本文构建面向用户级服务质量（Quality of Service，QoS）保障的分布式协同资源管控体系，围绕端到端用户面承载构建与业务内容可靠传输两个层面展开研究。网络状态感知、接入切换和无线资源调度分别提供可靠局部网络视图、维持用户--卫星服务关联和按需配置时频资源块，共同构建保障业务速率、时延和分组传输可靠性的端到端用户面承载；在此可靠承载基础上，任务信息可靠恢复利用任务反馈与协同增量重传保障业务内容的可靠传输。

（1）针对邻居状态缺失、陈旧或含噪导致本地多跳网络视图不准确、分布式路由状态输入不可靠的问题，提出多源置信度调控的分布式网络状态感知（CA-LDP）框架。该框架联合译码观测、时序先验、邻域空间支撑和信息新鲜度，通过置信度自适应图信号修复校准不准确邻域状态，并以置信度加权多跳传播抑制低可信状态扩散。在 1584 颗卫星的 Starlink 星座中，CA-LDP 在轻载和中载六个负载--BER 工作点相对各工作点最优基线的有效传输速率平均提高 $7.47\%$；在全部九个工作点的端到端时延平均降低 $4.75\%$，业务传输成功率均为 $100\%$。

（2）针对高动态多重覆盖下目标卫星、随机接入资源与子信道竞争耦合导致弱服务用户难以维持接入服务连续性的问题，提出基于局部公平性估计的分布式协作接入切换（FG-MAPPO）机制。该机制以用户长期累计接入效用为低开销邻域协作信令，结合广义 Gini 公平聚合函数构造的修正训练信号与吞吐优先--公平修正（TOFO）分立结构，使终端仅依赖本地观测和有限邻域信令即可兼顾候选链路质量、负载状态与长期公平性。FG-MAPPO 兼容 3GPP 条件切换流程，仅交互邻域信息时，相较具备全局子信道状态的集中式负载均衡切换策略，尾部加权广义 Gini 公平性指标和平均速率分别提高 $3.48\%$ 和 $6.91\%$；相较用户不协作的独立强化学习切换方法，切换成功率提高 $15.65\%$，星间负载方差降低 $30.91\%$。单跳的邻域感知信令开销上限为 $7.73$ KiB/s。

（3）针对业务突发、信道波动和用户竞争下候选切片预算对长期用户级 QoS 的支撑能力难以前瞻评估的问题，提出基于生成式网络状态感知的切片--MAC 分层协作优化（DTPS）框架。切片层通过生成式网络状态感知模型学习候选预算下的未来 QoS 演化，为 MAC 层配置长期资源预算；MAC 层估计业务流达到综合 QoS 效用门限所需的最小 RBG--功率开销，筛选 QoS 可行业务流，并依据由 QCI/5QI 标识导出的业务优先级执行资源映射。Starlink 星座仿真中，过载业务下 DTPS 的用户级 QoS 满足率为 $80.8\%$，TDRL-RSUA、RL-JRSS、LACO 与 DQN-MAC 四种基线为 $33.0\%$--$56.3\%$；相较 TDRL-RSUA 基线提高 $47.8$ 个百分点，平均时延为 $78.3$~ms。突发不确定性增强时，其 QoS 满足率仍保持在 $97.6\%$ 以上。

（4）针对星上算力受限时轻量语义编码器单次传输难以保证地面任务性能的问题，提出基于任务反馈与协同增量重传的任务信息可靠恢复（CIS-R）机制。该机制无需在星上发送端部署下游任务模型，由地面接收端根据当前任务判决对分块级接收表征的敏感度反馈后续传输优先级，星上发送端生成所选分块的语义增量，地面接收端跨轮累积接收表征，以有限增量通信开销换取任务性能。高压缩率图像传输中，三轮增量传输将 ImageNet-100 的 Top-1 分类准确率提高 $12.24$ 个百分点；遥感卫星过顶北京密云、喀什和三亚地面站的星地图像传输仿真中，在前向传输与反馈开销相同时，任务驱动反馈相较随机反馈将双任务平均分类准确率分别提高 $5.4$、$5.4$ 和 $4.5$ 个百分点。CIS-R 通信模型参数量仅约为生成式 DiffJSCC 方案的 $1/2500$。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract}[en]
Under the IMT-2030 6G vision, regenerative payloads and inter-satellite links are driving low Earth orbit (LEO) satellite networks from complementary coverage systems toward space infrastructure for non-terrestrial access, inter-satellite backhaul, and global service delivery, while shifting constellation management from centralized ground control to autonomous onboard decision-making. Under the constraints of rapidly varying topology, uneven spatiotemporal traffic distribution, limited inter-satellite backhaul capacity, and constrained onboard resources in large-scale LEO constellations, this dissertation develops a distributed cooperative resource management framework for user-level quality of service (QoS) assurance at two levels: end-to-end user-plane bearer establishment and reliable service-content delivery. Network-state awareness, access and handover, and wireless resource scheduling provide reliable local network views, maintain user--satellite service associations, and allocate time--frequency resource blocks on demand, respectively, thereby jointly establishing an end-to-end user-plane bearer that supports data-rate, latency, and packet-delivery reliability requirements. Over this reliable bearer, task-information recovery uses task feedback and coordinated incremental retransmission to ensure reliable service-content delivery.

(1) To address inaccurate local multihop network views and unreliable state inputs for distributed routing caused by missing, stale, or noisy neighbor states, this dissertation proposes a distributed network-state awareness framework with multi-source confidence regulation (CA-LDP). The framework combines decoded observations, temporal priors, neighborhood spatial support, and information freshness to calibrate inaccurate neighbor states through confidence-adaptive graph-signal restoration, and suppresses the propagation of low-confidence states through confidence-weighted multihop propagation. In a 1584-satellite Starlink constellation, CA-LDP improves the effective transmission rate by an average of $7.47\%$ over the best baseline at each of six load--BER operating points under light and medium loads. Across all nine operating points, it reduces the end-to-end delay by an average of $4.75\%$ while achieving a $100\%$ traffic delivery success ratio.

(2) To address the difficulty of maintaining access-service continuity for users with poor service quality due to coupled contention for target satellites, random-access resources, and subchannels under highly dynamic multi-coverage, this dissertation proposes a distributed cooperative access and handover mechanism based on local fairness estimation (FG-MAPPO). FG-MAPPO uses each user's long-term cumulative access utility as low-overhead neighborhood-coordination signaling and combines a fairness-corrected training signal constructed using generalized Gini aggregation with a separate throughput-oriented and fairness-oriented (TOFO) structure. This enables terminals to account for candidate-link quality, load, and long-term fairness using only local observations and limited neighborhood signaling. FG-MAPPO is compatible with the 3GPP Conditional Handover procedure. With only neighborhood information exchanged, it improves the tail-weighted generalized Gini fairness metric and the average rate by $3.48\%$ and $6.91\%$, respectively, relative to centralized load-balancing handover with global subchannel-state information. Relative to noncooperative independent reinforcement-learning handover, it improves the handover success ratio by $15.65\%$ and reduces inter-satellite load variance by $30.91\%$. The signaling overhead for single-hop neighborhood awareness is at most $7.73$ KiB/s.

(3) To address the difficulty of proactively assessing whether candidate slice budgets can support long-term user-level QoS under traffic bursts, channel fluctuations, and user contention, this dissertation proposes a slice--MAC hierarchical cooperative optimization framework based on generative network-state awareness (DTPS). The slice layer uses a generative network-state awareness model to learn future QoS evolution under candidate budgets and configures long-term resource budgets for the MAC layer. The MAC layer estimates the minimum RBG--power cost required for a flow to reach the composite QoS utility threshold, selects QoS-feasible flows, and maps resources according to service priorities derived from QCI/5QI identifiers. Starlink constellation simulations show that under overloaded traffic, DTPS achieves a user-level QoS satisfaction ratio of $80.8\%$, whereas TDRL-RSUA, RL-JRSS, LACO, and DQN-MAC achieve $33.0\%$--$56.3\%$. Relative to TDRL-RSUA, DTPS improves the QoS satisfaction ratio by $47.8$ percentage points and yields an average delay of $78.3$~ms. Its QoS satisfaction ratio remains above $97.6\%$ as burst uncertainty increases.

(4) To address the difficulty of ensuring task performance at the ground receiver with a single transmission from a lightweight semantic encoder under constrained onboard computing resources, this dissertation proposes a reliable task-information recovery mechanism based on task feedback and coordinated incremental retransmission (CIS-R). The mechanism requires no downstream task model at the satellite transmitter. The ground receiver feeds back subsequent transmission priorities according to the sensitivity of the current task decision to block-level received representations; the satellite transmitter generates semantic increments for the selected blocks; and the ground receiver accumulates received representations across rounds, thereby trading limited incremental communication overhead for improved task performance. Under high-compression-ratio image transmission, three incremental transmission rounds improve the Top-1 classification accuracy on ImageNet-100 by $12.24$ percentage points. In satellite--ground image transmission simulations during remote-sensing satellite passes over the Miyun (Beijing), Kashgar, and Sanya ground stations, under identical forward-transmission and feedback overheads, task-driven feedback improves the mean classification accuracy across two tasks by $5.4$, $5.4$, and $4.5$ percentage points, respectively, relative to random feedback. The CIS-R communication model contains only about $1/2500$ as many parameters as the generative DiffJSCC scheme.
\end{abstract}
