\begin{abstract}[zh]
在 IMT-2030 所描绘的第六代移动通信（6G）愿景中，低轨（Low Earth Orbit，LEO）卫星网络凭借广覆盖、较低传输时延与高容量等特性，正成为支撑非地面接入、星间回传与全球连续服务的关键基础设施。随着小型化载荷与星上计算能力的持续发展，人工智能（Artificial Intelligence，AI）驱动的网络管控正由地面离线优化向星上在线决策演进。在此趋势下，LEO 星座不再仅充当透明转发管道，而需演进为具备服务质量（Quality of Service，QoS）保障能力的核心承载网，以支撑 6G 空天地一体化网络中的端到端业务承载。

面向 QoS 的端到端传输需要在三个维度上实现联合保障：状态感知与业务传输的可靠性、受限资源下的资源配置效率，以及多用户竞争下的接入公平性。基于 AI 的星上分布式管控以本地决策与邻域协同替代全局状态同步与集中求解，为此提供了具有前景的技术范式；然而在去中心化条件下，上述三个维度沿“感知—接入—调度—传输”路径具体化为四重难题：其一，退化遥测使局部观测的可用性与补全状态的可信度难以兼顾，且恢复残差的不确定性沿多跳聚合扩散，节点难以构建可信的本地多跳网络视图；其二，缺乏中心协调的海量并发切换使用户个体的短期接入收益与系统级长期公平性、负载均衡相互冲突，须在受限邻域信令条件下在线化解；其三，多用户竞争与业务突发使资源决策的用户级 QoS 后果滞后且随机波动，受限的物理资源易被配置给 QoS 不可达的用户，用户级长期 QoS 难以保障；其四，星上资源受限使发送端难以通过提升模型复杂度增强语义传输的可靠性。上述四重难题分别对应状态可靠性、接入公平性、资源效率与传输可靠性。由此，如何在缺乏可靠全局状态与中心化协调的条件下，沿端到端路径协同保障可靠性、效率与公平性，构成本文的核心问题。为此，本文提出一套可在星上在线执行的分布式协同资源管控方法体系，以本地状态校准、有限邻域交互与跨节点协作为基础，将端到端 QoS 保障沿“感知—接入—调度—传输”路径分解为可信状态感知、公平协作切换、切片—MAC 跨层调度与语义可靠传输四项关键技术，共同构成面向大规模 LEO 星座的闭环管控体系。本文的主要工作与创新点如下：

（1）在面向鲁棒性的网络状态感知方面，邻居状态的陈旧与缺失会污染状态聚合中的可靠信息，削弱路由决策的可靠性。为此，本文提出置信度自适应分布式处理（Confidence-Adaptive Local Distributed Processing，CA-LDP）框架，实现动态自适应的图信号恢复与置信度调制的图信号聚合。其中，置信度自适应图信号修复（Confidence-Adaptive Graph Signal Restoration，CaGSR）将退化遥测的恢复建模为时间先验、空间相关性与最新观测三类信息源之间的信用分配，在时空相关性与信息新鲜度之间进行权衡，据此校准陈旧与缺失的遥测数据。为抑制信息传播过程中低置信节点对可靠节点的污染，置信度加权多跳传播机制将状态聚合建模为图上的反应—扩散过程，由节点置信度调控信息扩散强度：发送端依据置信度控制自身状态向邻居扩散的强度，接收端依据置信度保持自身的可靠状态。在由 1584 颗卫星组成的真实 Starlink 星座与九类受控遥测退化场景下，CA-LDP 相较代表性基线将平均传输速率提升 $4.8\%$--$10.8\%$，将端到端时延降低 $6.7\%$--$11.1\%$，并保持接近 $100\%$ 的路由成功率。上述结果表明，CA-LDP 能够在退化遥测条件下增强分布式路由的鲁棒性。

（2）在面向服务连续性保障的移动性管理方面，海量用户基于局部观测的并发切换使用户个体容量最大化与系统级接入公平性相互冲突。为此，本文提出公平性引导的多智能体近端策略优化（Fairness-Guided Multi-Agent Proximal Policy Optimization，FG-MAPPO）方法，将系统级公平目标转化为可由用户本地估计的优势修正量，从而在无全局协调的条件下实现公平感知的协作切换。该方法以广义 Gini 社会福利函数（Generalized Gini Social Welfare Function）统一刻画吞吐效率与接入公平性，构建多目标马尔可夫决策过程（Multi-Objective Markov Decision Process，MOMDP）并推导其策略梯度，证明各用户仅需在邻域内广播经社会福利函数修正的个体优势估计，即可以低开销估计邻域公平性。在此基础上，本文设计吞吐与公平分立（Throughput-Oriented and Fairness-Oriented，TOFO）的网络结构与两阶段训练机制，以降低多目标价值估计的方差，并构建与 3GPP 条件切换（Conditional Handover，CHO）流程兼容的分布式同步训练框架。仿真结果表明，在仅交互邻域信息的条件下，FG-MAPPO 的广义 Gini 公平性指标（Generalized Gini Fairness，GGF）较具备全局信息的集中式匈牙利算法提升 $3.48\%$，平均速率提升 $6.91\%$；相较分布式强化学习基线，其切换成功率与负载均衡性能最高分别提升 $15.65\%$ 与 $30.91\%$。上述结果验证了局部公平性估计与分布式协作切换的有效性。

（3）在面向资源高效的用户级 QoS 保障方面，多用户竞争与业务突发使资源决策的用户级 QoS 后果滞后且随机波动：一次切片层预算须经后续多个时隙的 MAC 层竞争、业务到达与信道起伏之后，才反映为用户级 QoS 满足与否，且聚合吞吐并不等价于用户级 QoS。资源高效的用户级 QoS 保障由此面临两个科学问题：其一，滞后的资源决策难以抵御环境的随机波动，须增强对网络环境演化动态的表达能力，使预算决策得以预判其长期 QoS 后果；其二，面向长期 QoS 保障的跨层联合优化呈高维混合整数形式，难以在星上在线求解。针对上述问题，本文提出双时间尺度预测调度（Dual-Timescale Predictive Scheduling，DTPS）框架，以带宽与功率预算为跨层接口、以 QoS 可行性为切片层与 MAC 层协同的核心准则，将长期 QoS 保障的高维优化分解为切片层的 QoS 可行域保障与 MAC 层的实时调度。在切片层，本文设计基于世界模型（World Model）的生成式切片资源预留机制以增强对环境动态的表达能力，学习预算条件下的用户级 QoS 可行性动态而非仅预测业务需求：后验网络融合真实 MAC 层反馈校正隐空间表征，先验网络自记忆生成想象的未来切片轨迹，使切片策略无需在线多步随机规划即可评估预算决策的长期效应；在线执行时以最新 MAC 层反馈进行后验校正，缓解突发条件下的隐状态漂移，并以带宽与功率预算界定 MAC 层调度的 QoS 可行域。在 MAC 层，本文设计服务质量类别标识（QoS Class Identifier，QCI）优先级背包调度器，在切片预算界定的可行域内执行实时调度，将无线资源调度由吞吐或紧迫度驱动重构为预算约束下的 QoS 可行性感知选择：先估计各候选流满足其速率—时延联合门限所需的最小资源块组（Resource Block Group，RBG）—功率开销并滤除不可行流，再以 QCI/5QI 导出优先级为物品价值，经二维背包在固定切片预算下选取优先级加权的可行流子集。在 Starlink 规模星座仿真中，DTPS 在过载业务下实现 $80.8\%$ 的用户级 QoS 满足率，显著高于代表性基线的 $33.0\%$--$56.3\%$，并将平均时延保持在 $78.3$~ms；突发不确定性增强时其 QoS 满足率始终维持在 $97.6\%$ 以上。上述结果表明，基于世界模型的预测式预算控制能够在业务与信道不确定性下保障用户级长期 QoS，可行性感知的跨层联合调度则将受限的星上带宽与功率分配至 QoS 可达的业务流，实现了资源高效的用户级 QoS 保障。

（4）在面向资源受限下的可靠语义传输与差错控制方面，LEO 卫星的端到端长传输时延与星上载荷算力受限，使语义编解码须在模型复杂度与传输效率之间进行权衡；同时，星地链路呈现“轻量发送端—任务感知接收端”的非对称结构，发送端仅采集并转发图像，而异构下游任务运行于接收端，难以为每类任务部署任务专用编解码器。现有高性能语义图像传输方法多以增大模型规模换取精度，依赖任务专用语义编码器、大参数量 Transformer 联合信源信道编码（Joint Source-Channel Coding，JSCC）或生成式复原模型，其计算、存储与时延开销难以适配星上受限载荷。为此，本文转向“以扩展通信替代扩展星上计算”的设计思路，提出协同增量语义混合自动重传请求（Coordinated Incremental Semantic Hybrid Automatic Repeat Request，CIS-HARQ）机制，将每一轮 HARQ 由完整语义重传重构为反馈引导的选择性语义精化，使轻量发送端以少量反馈条件化的重传轮次换取模型容量，在不于星上部署下游任务模型的前提下逐步逼近高复杂度模型的任务性能。在每一轮交互中，任务感知接收端在严格的反馈预算下，依据各语义分量对任务判决裕度的贡献定位最关键的语义分量，并将其压缩为紧凑的 Top-$K$ 硬掩码，经低速率控制信令回传至发送端；发送端结合 HARQ 轮次嵌入，由反馈条件化增量编码器经空间特征变换生成码率受约束的选择性语义增量，避免重复发送完整的语义表征；接收端再以门控软合并与掩码引导的语义累积仅更新被请求的分量，在保留有用历史语义、抑制含噪与冗余增量的同时，于前向链路、反馈链路与模型复杂度三重约束下逐步恢复任务相关语义。由于计算开销较大的梯度式反馈生成驻留于资源充裕的地面接收端，星上发送端始终保持轻量。在 ImageNet-100、CIFAR-100 与 VeRi-776 数据集、Rician 衰落信道、信噪比 $5$ dB 且信道带宽比（Channel Bandwidth Ratio，CBR）约为 $0.02$ 的紧带宽条件下，CIS-HARQ 经三轮增量传输将下游任务精度由同带宽基线的 $35.7\%$ 提升至 $65.0\%$；其参数量仅约 $2.2$ M、星上推理时延为毫秒级，相较生成式扩散接收端参数量降低约 $2500$ 倍、推理时延由秒级降至毫秒级，任务精度差距控制在约 $7$ 个百分点以内，并在不同数据分布与多任务接收端上保持一致的精化增益。

综上，本文以分布式协同决策为主线，以状态可信与传输可靠为前提，以效率与公平的协同权衡为核心，构建了覆盖可信感知、公平接入、高效调度与可靠传输的闭环管控体系。四项关键技术并非孤立的分层优化，而是沿“感知—接入—调度—传输”路径形成“前提—核心”的协同关系：一方面，可信网络状态感知与语义可靠传输分别在路径的输入侧与输出侧确立可靠性前提，前者保障路由决策所依赖的网络状态可信，后者以低开销为业务传输提供差错恢复能力，二者共同构成效率与公平优化的可信基础；另一方面，公平协作切换与双时间尺度预测调度在此基础上协同求解效率与公平的核心权衡，前者在无全局协调的条件下保障连续接入与多用户长期公平，后者将切片级预算转化为受限资源下的用户级 QoS 保障。星座规模的仿真与语义传输实验表明，所提机制在网络状态可信感知、接入公平性、过载业务下的用户级 QoS 满足率以及紧带宽下的任务精度等方面均取得显著增益。本文将大规模 LEO 星座的资源管控由分层独立优化推进至面向端到端 QoS 的分布式协同管控，为 6G 空天地一体化网络的星上边缘自治提供了理论方法与可验证的技术路径。

\begin{abstract}[en]
In the IMT-2030 vision of sixth-generation mobile communications (6G), low Earth orbit (LEO) satellite networks are emerging as a key infrastructure for non-terrestrial access, inter-satellite backhaul, and global service continuity, owing to their wide coverage, relatively low transmission latency, and high capacity. With the advancement of miniaturized payloads and onboard computing capabilities, artificial intelligence (AI)-driven network management is evolving from ground-based offline optimization toward onboard online decision-making. However, unlike terrestrial cellular networks, which perform centralized control by relying on stable topologies, reliable backhaul, and observable global states, LEO constellations are subject to inherent constraints, including high mobility, degraded telemetry, unreliable backhaul, and limited onboard resources, and therefore cannot directly adopt the centralized paradigm of terrestrial networks. Accordingly, the control paradigm of satellite networks is shifting from ground-centric centralized scheduling toward onboard autonomy and neighborhood-level coordination. Under this trend, LEO constellations are no longer expected to serve merely as transparent forwarding pipes; instead, they must evolve into autonomous networks with quality-of-service (QoS) assurance capabilities to support end-to-end service provisioning in 6G space-air-ground integrated networks.

End-to-end transmission with QoS assurance requires joint guarantees along three dimensions: the reliability of network-state perception and service transmission, the efficiency of resource allocation under limited resources, and the fairness of access under multi-user contention. AI-based onboard distributed management, which replaces global-state synchronization and centralized computation with local decision-making and neighborhood coordination, offers a promising paradigm for this purpose. Under decentralized control, however, these three dimensions, when projected onto the ``perception--access--scheduling--transmission'' path, manifest as four fundamental difficulties intrinsic to LEO constellations. First, degraded telemetry makes the availability of local observations and the trustworthiness of restored states difficult to reconcile, and the uncertainty of the restoration residual propagates along multi-hop aggregation, preventing nodes from constructing a trustworthy local multi-hop network view. Second, massive concurrent handovers without centralized coordination cause the short-term access gain of individual users to conflict with system-level long-term fairness and load balancing, and this conflict must be resolved online under limited neighborhood signaling. Third, multi-user contention and traffic bursts render the user-level QoS consequences of resource decisions delayed and stochastic, so that the limited physical resources are easily allocated to users whose QoS targets are unattainable and long-term user-level QoS can hardly be guaranteed. Fourth, the limited onboard resources prevent the satellite transmitter from improving the reliability of semantic transmission by scaling up model complexity. These four difficulties correspond to the requirements of state reliability, access fairness, resource efficiency, and transmission reliability, respectively. Therefore, the core problem addressed in this dissertation is how to jointly guarantee reliability, efficiency, and fairness along the end-to-end transmission path in the absence of reliable global states and centralized coordination.

Building on local state calibration, limited neighborhood interaction, and cross-node cooperation, this dissertation develops a suite of distributed cooperative resource-management methods that can be executed online onboard satellites, thereby reducing the dependence on global-state synchronization and centralized decision-making. Along the ``perception--access--scheduling--transmission'' path, end-to-end QoS assurance is decomposed into four key techniques, namely trustworthy state perception, fair cooperative handover, slice--MAC cross-layer scheduling, and reliable semantic transmission, which jointly form a closed-loop resource-management framework for large-scale LEO constellations. The main contributions of this dissertation are summarized as follows.

(1) For robust network-state perception, stale and missing neighbor states contaminate the trustworthy information during state aggregation, thereby degrading the reliability of routing decisions. To address this issue, this dissertation proposes a Confidence-Adaptive Local Distributed Processing (CA-LDP) framework that performs dynamically adaptive graph-signal restoration and confidence-modulated graph-signal aggregation. Within CA-LDP, Confidence-Adaptive Graph Signal Restoration (CaGSR) casts the recovery of degraded telemetry as a credit assignment among three information sources, namely the temporal prior, the spatial correlation, and the latest observation, and balances spatio-temporal correlation against information freshness to calibrate stale and missing telemetry. To prevent low-confidence nodes from contaminating reliable nodes during information propagation, a confidence-weighted multi-hop propagation mechanism models state aggregation as a reaction--diffusion process on the graph, in which the node confidence regulates the intensity of information diffusion: at the sender side, the confidence determines how strongly the state of a node diffuses to its neighbors, whereas at the receiver side, the confidence determines how strongly a node retains its own reliable state. Under nine controlled telemetry-degradation scenarios on a real Starlink constellation comprising 1584 satellites, CA-LDP improves the average transmission rate by $4.8\%$--$10.8\%$, reduces the end-to-end delay by $6.7\%$--$11.1\%$, and maintains a routing success ratio close to $100\%$, compared with representative baselines. These results demonstrate that CA-LDP enhances the robustness of distributed routing under degraded telemetry.

(2) For mobility management toward service-continuity assurance, massive concurrent handovers driven by local observations cause the maximization of individual user capacity to conflict with system-level access fairness. To address this issue, this dissertation proposes a Fairness-Guided Multi-Agent Proximal Policy Optimization (FG-MAPPO) method, which converts the system-level fairness objective into an advantage-correction term that each user can estimate locally, thereby enabling fairness-aware cooperative handover without global coordination. Specifically, a Generalized Gini Social Welfare Function is adopted to jointly characterize throughput efficiency and access fairness, and a Multi-Objective Markov Decision Process (MOMDP) is formulated, whose policy gradient is derived to prove that each user needs only to broadcast its social-welfare-corrected individual advantage estimate within its neighborhood to achieve low-overhead estimation of neighborhood fairness. On this basis, a Throughput-Oriented and Fairness-Oriented (TOFO) network architecture and a two-stage training mechanism are designed to reduce the variance of multi-objective value estimation, and a distributed synchronous training framework compatible with the 3GPP Conditional Handover (CHO) procedure is developed. Simulation results show that, with only neighborhood information exchange, FG-MAPPO improves the Generalized Gini Fairness (GGF) metric by $3.48\%$ and the average rate by $6.91\%$ over a centralized Hungarian algorithm with global information. Compared with distributed reinforcement-learning baselines, it improves the handover success rate and the load-balancing performance by up to $15.65\%$ and $30.91\%$, respectively. These results verify the effectiveness of local fairness estimation and distributed cooperative handover.

(3) For resource-efficient user-level QoS assurance, multi-user contention and traffic bursts render the user-level QoS consequences of resource decisions delayed and stochastic: a slice-level budget manifests itself as user-level QoS satisfaction only after MAC-layer contention, traffic arrivals, and channel fluctuations over multiple subsequent slots, and aggregate throughput is not equivalent to user-level QoS. Resource-efficient user-level QoS assurance therefore faces two scientific problems: first, the delayed resource decisions can hardly withstand the random fluctuations of the environment, which calls for an enhanced capability to represent the evolution dynamics of the network environment so that budget decisions can foresee their long-term QoS consequences; second, the cross-layer joint optimization for long-term QoS assurance takes a high-dimensional mixed-integer form that can hardly be solved online onboard. To this end, this dissertation proposes a Dual-Timescale Predictive Scheduling (DTPS) framework, which takes bandwidth and power budgets as the cross-layer interface and QoS feasibility as the core criterion for slice--MAC coordination, and decomposes the high-dimensional optimization for long-term QoS assurance into QoS feasible-region assurance at the slice layer and real-time scheduling at the MAC layer. At the slice layer, a world-model-based generative slice resource reservation mechanism enhances the representation of environment dynamics by learning the budget-conditioned dynamics of user-level QoS feasibility rather than merely forecasting traffic demand: a posterior network incorporates real MAC-layer feedback to calibrate the latent representation, whereas a prior network generates imagined future slice trajectories from memory, so that the slicing policy can evaluate the long-term effect of budget decisions without online multi-step stochastic planning; during online execution, posterior correction with the latest MAC-layer feedback mitigates latent-state drift under bursty conditions, and the bandwidth and power budgets delimit the QoS feasible region for MAC-layer scheduling. Within the feasible region delimited by the slice budgets, a QoS Class Identifier (QCI) priority knapsack scheduler at the MAC layer performs real-time scheduling, recasting radio resource scheduling from throughput- or urgency-driven allocation into budget-constrained QoS-feasibility-aware selection: it first estimates the minimum resource block group (RBG)--power cost required for each candidate flow to meet its joint rate--delay threshold and filters out infeasible flows, and then solves a two-dimensional knapsack problem that selects a priority-weighted subset of feasible flows under the fixed slice budgets, with QCI/5QI-derived priorities as item values. In Starlink-scale simulations, DTPS achieves a user-level QoS satisfaction ratio of $80.8\%$ under overloaded traffic, substantially outperforming representative baselines, which achieve only $33.0\%$--$56.3\%$, while keeping the average delay at $78.3$~ms; as the burst-induced uncertainty intensifies, its QoS satisfaction ratio remains above $97.6\%$. These results demonstrate that the world-model-based predictive budget control sustains long-term user-level QoS under traffic and channel uncertainty, and that the feasibility-aware cross-layer scheduling allocates the limited onboard bandwidth and power to the service flows whose QoS targets are attainable, thereby achieving resource-efficient user-level QoS assurance.

(4) For reliable semantic transmission and error control under resource constraints, the long end-to-end transmission latency and the limited onboard computing capability of LEO satellites force the semantic codec to trade off model complexity against transmission efficiency. Moreover, the space--ground link exhibits an asymmetric structure, in which a lightweight transmitter only acquires and forwards imagery while heterogeneous downstream tasks run at a task-aware receiver, so that hosting a task-specific codec for every task onboard is infeasible. Existing high-performance semantic image transmission schemes improve accuracy mainly by enlarging model capacity, relying on task-specific semantic encoders, large Transformer-based joint source-channel coding (JSCC), or generative restoration models, whose computation, memory, and latency costs can hardly be accommodated by resource-constrained satellite payloads. Following the design principle of scaling communication instead of scaling onboard computation, this dissertation proposes a Coordinated Incremental Semantic Hybrid Automatic Repeat Request (CIS-HARQ) mechanism, which recasts each HARQ round from a retransmission of the complete semantic representation into feedback-guided selective semantic refinement, so that a lightweight transmitter trades a few feedback-conditioned retransmission rounds for model capacity and progressively approaches the task performance of high-complexity models without hosting any downstream task model onboard. In each round, the task-aware receiver, under a stringent feedback budget, evaluates the contribution of each semantic component to the task decision margin, locates the most critical components, compresses them into a compact Top-$K$ hard mask, and returns the mask to the transmitter over a low-rate control channel. Conditioned on this mask and the HARQ-round embedding, the transmitter employs a feedback-conditioned incremental encoder with spatial feature transform to generate rate-constrained selective semantic increments, thereby avoiding repeated transmission of the complete semantic representation. The receiver then applies gated soft combining and mask-guided semantic accumulation to update only the requested components, preserving useful historical semantics while suppressing noisy and redundant increments, and thereby progressively recovers task-relevant semantics under the joint constraints of the forward link, the feedback link, and the model complexity. Because the computation-intensive gradient-based feedback generation resides at the resource-rich ground receiver, the onboard transmitter remains lightweight throughout. On the ImageNet-100, CIFAR-100, and VeRi-776 datasets over a Rician fading channel, under an SNR of $5$ dB and a tight channel bandwidth ratio (CBR) of approximately $0.02$, CIS-HARQ improves the downstream task accuracy from $35.7\%$ for the equal-bandwidth baseline to $65.0\%$ after three incremental rounds. With only about $2.2$ M parameters and millisecond-level onboard inference, it reduces the parameter count by a factor of about $2500$ relative to a generative diffusion-based receiver and shortens the inference time from seconds to milliseconds, while keeping the task accuracy gap within about $7$ percentage points and maintaining consistent refinement gains across datasets with different distributions and across multi-task receivers.

In summary, this dissertation takes distributed cooperative decision-making as the main thread, regards trustworthy states and reliable transmission as the prerequisite, and places the coordinated efficiency--fairness trade-off at the core, thereby constructing a closed-loop management framework that covers trustworthy perception, fair access, efficient scheduling, and reliable transmission. Rather than isolated layer-by-layer optimizations, the four key techniques form a ``prerequisite--core'' cooperative relationship along the ``perception--access--scheduling--transmission'' path. On the one hand, trustworthy network-state perception and reliable semantic transmission establish the reliability prerequisite at the input and output ends of the path: the former safeguards the trustworthiness of the network states on which routing decisions rely, whereas the latter provides low-overhead error recovery for service transmission, and the two together constitute the trustworthy foundation for the optimization of efficiency and fairness. On the other hand, building on this foundation, fairness-aware cooperative handover and dual-timescale predictive scheduling jointly resolve the core efficiency--fairness trade-off: the former sustains continuous access and long-term multi-user fairness without global coordination, whereas the latter translates slice-level budgets into user-level QoS guarantees under limited resources. Constellation-scale simulations and semantic transmission experiments show that the proposed mechanisms achieve significant gains in trustworthy network-state perception, access fairness, user-level QoS satisfaction under overloaded traffic, and task accuracy under tight bandwidth. This dissertation advances the resource management of large-scale LEO constellations from layered and isolated optimization toward end-to-end QoS-oriented distributed cooperative management, thereby providing theoretical methods and a verifiable technical path for onboard edge autonomy in 6G space-air-ground integrated networks.
