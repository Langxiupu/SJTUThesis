\begin{abstract}[zh]
在 IMT-2030 定义的第六代移动通信技术（6G）愿景中，泛在连接与万物智联是下一代信息基础设施的核心目标。低轨（Low Earth Orbit，LEO）卫星网络凭借广覆盖、低时延与高容量等特性，正成为 6G 体系中支撑非地面接入、星间回传与全球连续服务的重要基础设施。随着低成本小型化卫星载荷平台的演进，星上计算能力逐步增强，人工智能（Artificial Intelligence，AI）驱动的网络管控也由地面离线辅助优化向星上在线决策发展。相应地，卫星网络管控正由集中式调度向具备自感知、自决策、自优化能力的边缘自治模式转变。在此背景下，LEO 星座不再仅作为透明转发的中继，而需对多样化业务提供面向服务质量（Quality of Service，QoS）保障的端到端传输能力，这成为其深度融入 6G 体系的关键。

面向 QoS 的端到端传输本质上是一个跨协议层耦合的系统性问题，其性能由网络状态感知、接入与移动性管理、无线资源调度以及传输可靠性等多个环节共同决定，任一环节的局部最优均难以直接累积为端到端最优。在地面网络中，稳定的拓扑结构、可靠的回程链路与可获知的全局状态，使得集中式跨层优化得以有效实施；而在大规模 LEO 星座中，高速移动、遥测退化、不可靠回程与星上资源受限，导致全局状态难以实时、可靠地获取。此外，联合优化的决策空间随星座规模、用户规模和资源维度快速增长，且求解结果会因拓扑和信道的时变性而迅速失效。因此，依赖全局状态同步与集中计算的跨层资源优化在星座尺度上既难以求解，也难以部署。为此，本文以学习驱动的分布式协同调度为主线，沿协议栈将端到端资源调度分解为四个相互耦合的子问题：在网络层，解决退化遥测下分布式路由难以获得可靠网络状态的问题；在接入控制层，解决无全局协调下海量用户并发切换导致的接入竞争与公平性失衡问题；在接入网资源调度层，解决部分可观测性与随机业务突发下用户级 QoS 难以保障的问题；在传输可靠性层，解决星上算力受限和输入数据异构下语义反馈与重传开销过高的问题。上述四类子问题面临共同的核心难点，即跨层、跨节点或跨端信息难以全局获取、实时同步和可靠共享。针对这一难点，本文基于本地感知、有限反馈与节点间协作，设计低复杂度、可在线执行且具备环境自适应能力的分布式资源调度机制，使得各环节在缺乏可靠全局状态时仍能做出面向端到端 QoS 的前瞻性决策。本文的主要工作与创新点如下：

（1）在面向分布式路由的网络状态感知方面，针对退化遥测导致局部网络状态陈旧、缺失和置信度不一致，进而削弱分布式路由决策可靠性的问题，提出置信度自适应局部分布式处理框架（Confidence-Adaptive Local Distributed Processing，CA-LDP），为分布式路由构建可靠的本地多跳感知基础。CA-LDP 利用校准后置信度表征局部网络状态的可靠性，并将其贯穿于状态校准与多跳聚合全过程，使路由节点在利用局部观测的同时显式区分不同状态证据的可信程度。具体而言，本文设计了置信度自适应图信号修复方法（Confidence-Adaptive Graph Signal Restoration，CaGSR），通过观测—先验参考机制对陈旧与缺失的遥测数据进行校准，并生成校准后置信度。在此基础上，进一步构建置信度加权多跳传播机制，在发送端利用置信度权重抑制弱支撑状态的外扩传播，在接收端通过可靠证据锚定保留真实的局部拥塞信息，从而降低退化遥测在多跳特征构建中的误导作用，提升分布式路由在状态不可靠条件下的决策稳定性。该机制为端到端传输路径提供可靠的网络状态感知基础。在 Starlink 壳层星座与 9 类遥测退化场景下的仿真结果表明，相比于代表性基线方法，CA-LDP 将平均传输速率提升 4.8\%--10.8\%，端到端时延降低 6.7\%--11.1\%，并保持接近饱和的传输成功率。

（2）在接入与移动性管理方面，针对随机接入缺乏全局协调、海量用户并发切换引发接入竞争与用户间公平性失衡，进而破坏服务连续性的问题，提出公平引导的多智能体近端策略优化方法（Fairness-Guided Multi-Agent Proximal Policy Optimization，FG-MAPPO），在分布式协作条件下实现面向长期公平性的切换接入控制。该方法将系统公平目标转化为经广义 Gini 社会福利函数修正的优势估计，使用户仅依据邻域信息即可进行公平感知的切换决策。本文将用户接入公平性保障建模为最大化最小用户吞吐量的多目标优化问题，并构建多目标马尔可夫决策过程（Multi-Objective Markov Decision Process，MOMDP），以刻画切换行为对长期接入性能和系统公平性的累积影响。为解决人工权重设计缺乏可解释性的问题，引入广义 Gini 社会福利函数对吞吐效率与公平性进行标量化，并推导了多目标策略梯度的解析形式。基于该推导结果，证明了用户通过在邻域内广播经广义 Gini 社会福利函数修正的个体优势估计值，即可实现对邻域公平性的低开销估计。在此基础上，设计了吞吐与公平分立（TOFO）的网络结构，将吞吐量优化分支与公平性优化分支分离，并采用两阶段训练方式降低多目标价值估计的方差。最后，构建分布式同步训练框架以支撑大规模用户协作切换学习，并保持与 3GPP 条件切换（CHO）机制的兼容性。该机制在端到端传输路径的接入阶段实现了服务连续性与长期公平性的统一。仿真结果表明，在仅交互邻域信息的分布式条件下，FG-MAPPO 的广义 Gini 公平性指标（Generalized Gini Fairness，GGF）较具备全局信息的集中式匈牙利算法提升 3.48\%，平均速率提升 6.91\%，且相比于分布式强化学习基线方法，其切换成功率与负载均衡性能最高分别提升 15.65\% 和 30.91\%。

（3）在无线资源跨层调度方面，针对部分可观测性、随机业务突发和时变服务能力下，切片级预算控制与 MAC 级资源分配难以协同，导致用户级 QoS 难以保障和跨层联合优化难以直接求解的问题，提出双时间尺度预测调度框架（Dual-Timescale Predictive Scheduling，DTPS）。该框架以带宽与功率预算衔接慢时间尺度的切片级预算控制与快时间尺度的 MAC 级无线资源调度，并据此进行面向用户级 QoS 的选择性资源分配。在切片层，DTPS 通过环境动力学预测模型（World Model）学习预算约束下的 QoS 可行性状态演进规律，刻画不同带宽和功率预算对未来用户级 QoS 满足情况的影响。为应对部分可观测环境，构建先验—后验状态空间预测模型，并结合基于状态演进预测的策略学习与在线后验校正，使切片层能够在观测不完整和业务突发条件下生成具备前瞻性的预算控制决策。在 MAC 层，设计了基于 QoS 类别标识（QoS Class Identifier，QCI）优先级的二维背包调度器，将切片层给定的带宽和功率预算转化为资源块组（Resource Block Group，RBG）与功率的联合分配问题，并通过最小 RBG—功率可行性判定降低资源消耗。该机制以优先级加权 QoS 满意率为优化目标，在资源受限条件下提升了用户级 QoS 保障能力和无线资源利用效率。在 Starlink 规模化网络仿真中，当处于过载业务场景时，用户级 QoS 满足率达到 80.8\%，显著优于代表性方法的 33.0\%--56.3\%。

（4）在受限条件下的语义可靠传输方面，针对星上载荷算力受限、承载业务输入数据异构以及全量重传开销过高，导致语义反馈与重传难以低开销实现的问题，提出协同增量语义混合自动重传请求机制（Coordinated Incremental Semantic Hybrid Automatic Repeat Request，CIS-HARQ），通过轻量闭环增量传输实现端到端可靠语义交付。CIS-HARQ 不依赖发送端部署大参数量或专用语义评估模型，而是在接收端根据语义失真生成 top-$K$ 硬掩码反馈，并通过低速控制信令回传至发送端，从而将接收端的语义失真感知转化为发送端生成增量语义的决策依据。基于该反馈，设计了反馈条件化增量编码器，并引入 HARQ 轮次嵌入机制，使发送端能够根据历史反馈和当前重传轮次生成选择性语义增量，避免重复发送完整语义表示。在接收端，进一步设计了掩码引导的语义累积机制，将多轮接收的语义增量按照失真掩码进行融合与更新，从而在有限带宽和计算资源下逐步提升语义表征质量。该机制将反馈、增量编码与语义累积统一为闭环过程，在降低重传开销的同时增强异构输入数据下的端到端语义可靠性。实验结果表明，在 ImageNet-100 数据集、信噪比为 5 dB、信道带宽比（Channel Bandwidth Ratio，CBR）约为 0.02 的紧带宽条件下，CIS-HARQ 经三轮增量后将下游任务精度由同带宽基线方法的 35.7\% 提升至 65.0\%，并以远低于生成式基线方法的参数规模与传输时延取得性能增益。

综上，本文面向大规模 LEO 星座中缺乏可靠全局状态、中心化协调受限、星上资源受限条件下的用户级端到端 QoS 保障问题，提出了分布式协同资源调度方法体系。该体系沿协议栈围绕面向分布式路由的网络状态感知、接入与移动性管理、无线资源跨层调度以及受限条件下的语义可靠传输四个关键环节，分别构建低复杂度、自适应的学习驱动调度机制，实现了网络状态可信感知、接入公平控制、用户级 QoS 保障与语义可靠交付。星座规模仿真与语义传输实验表明，四项机制分别在路由性能、接入公平性、过载业务下用户级 QoS 满足率与紧带宽下任务精度方面取得显著增益，为大规模 LEO 星座从分层独立优化向面向端到端 QoS 的分布式协同管控转变提供了系统化解决方案。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract}[en]
In the IMT-2030 vision of sixth-generation (6G) mobile communications, ubiquitous connectivity and the intelligent interconnection of everything are core objectives of the next-generation information infrastructure. Owing to their wide coverage, low latency, and high capacity, low Earth orbit (LEO) satellite networks are becoming key infrastructure for non-terrestrial access, inter-satellite backhaul, and globally continuous services in 6G. As low-cost miniaturized payloads evolve and onboard computing grows, artificial intelligence (AI)–driven network control is shifting from ground-based offline optimization toward onboard online decision-making, moving satellite network control from centralized scheduling toward an edge-autonomous mode with self-perception, self-decision, and self-optimization. Learning-driven distributed decision-making thus offers a viable path to quality-of-service (QoS)–oriented end-to-end resource scheduling under constrained communication and computing.

In large-scale LEO networks, however, reliable real-time global state is difficult to obtain, and the onboard sensing, communication, and computing resources are tightly constrained, so conventional centralized scheduling that relies on global-state synchronization or high-complexity computation is impractical. End-to-end QoS provisioning is a cross-layer problem that couples network-state perception and distributed routing, access and mobility management, cross-layer radio resource scheduling, and semantic-reliable transmission under limited resources. In terrestrial networks, stable topology, reliable backhaul, and observable global state make centralized cross-layer optimization feasible; in large-scale LEO constellations, high mobility, telemetry degradation, unreliable backhaul, and limited onboard resources invalidate these premises. On the one hand, cross-layer and cross-node states cannot be acquired reliably in real time; on the other hand, the joint decision space expands rapidly with constellation size, user population, and resource dimensions, while centralized solutions become outdated under time-varying topology and channels. Centralized cross-layer optimization is therefore neither tractable nor deployable at constellation scale.

Under these constraints, this dissertation recasts cross-layer resource scheduling as a set of locally executable distributed learning decisions with a unified objective. Along the protocol stack and the end-to-end path, four resource-control problems are addressed: at the network layer, unreliable network-state perception for distributed routing under degraded telemetry; at the access-control layer, access contention and fairness imbalance under massive concurrent handovers without global coordination; at the radio access layer, user-level QoS provisioning under partial observability and bursty traffic; and at the transmission-reliability layer, the high feedback and retransmission cost of semantic delivery under limited onboard computing and heterogeneous inputs. These problems share a common difficulty, namely that cross-layer and cross-node states are not globally available, synchronizable, or fully reliable, and are addressed by a unified principle: extracting from local observations compact decision features strongly correlated with end-to-end QoS—network-state confidence, user fairness utility, QoS feasibility, and semantic distortion—and using them to drive foresighted local resource allocation. The main contributions are summarized as follows.

(1) For network-state perception and distributed routing, to counter the stale, missing, and confidence-inconsistent local states under degraded telemetry that undermine the reliability of routing decisions, a Confidence-Adaptive Local Distributed Processing (CA-LDP) framework is proposed to build a reliable local multi-hop perception basis for distributed routing. CA-LDP uses a post-calibration confidence to characterize the reliability of local network states and carries it through state calibration and multi-hop aggregation, so that routing nodes exploit local observations while explicitly distinguishing the credibility of different state evidence. Specifically, a Confidence-Adaptive Graph Signal Restoration (CaGSR) method calibrates stale and missing telemetry through an observation–prior reference and produces the post-calibration confidence; a confidence-weighted multi-hop propagation mechanism then suppresses the outward diffusion of weakly supported states at the sender side and anchors reliable local congestion evidence at the receiver side, reducing the misleading effect of degraded telemetry in multi-hop feature construction and improving the stability of routing decisions under unreliable states. On a Starlink-shell topology under nine telemetry-degradation scenarios, CA-LDP improves the average transmission rate by 4.8\%--10.8\% and reduces the end-to-end delay by 6.7\%--11.1\% over the strongest baseline, while maintaining a near-saturated delivery ratio.

(2) For access and mobility management, to address the access contention and user-QoS imbalance caused by massive concurrent handovers under random access without global coordination, a Fairness-Guided Multi-Agent Proximal Policy Optimization (FG-MAPPO) method is proposed to realize long-term-fairness-oriented handover access control under distributed cooperation. The method converts the system fairness objective into a fairness-utility-corrected advantage estimate, so that users make fairness-aware distributed handover decisions while exchanging only neighborhood information. Access fairness is formulated as a max-min user-throughput multi-objective optimization problem and modeled as a multi-objective Markov decision process (MOMDP) to capture the cumulative effect of handovers on long-term access performance and system fairness. To overcome the poor interpretability of manual weighting, a generalized Gini fairness-utility function scalarizes throughput efficiency and fairness, and the analytical form of the multi-objective policy gradient is derived; based on this result, it is shown that broadcasting fairness-utility-corrected individual advantage estimates within a neighborhood enables low-overhead neighborhood-fairness estimation. A throughput-and-fairness-decoupled (TOFO) network structure with two-stage training reduces the variance of multi-objective value estimation, and a distributed synchronous training framework supports large-scale cooperative handover learning while remaining compatible with the 3GPP conditional handover (CHO) procedure. Exchanging only neighborhood information, FG-MAPPO improves the fairness metric (GGF) by 3.48\% and the average rate by 6.91\% over a centralized Hungarian algorithm with global knowledge, and improves handover success and load balancing by up to 15.65\% and 30.91\% over distributed reinforcement-learning baselines.

(3) For cross-layer radio resource scheduling, to address the difficulty of coordinating slice-level budgeting and MAC-level allocation under partial observability, bursty traffic, and time-varying service capability, which makes user-level QoS hard to guarantee and the cross-layer joint problem hard to solve directly, a Dual-Timescale Predictive Scheduling (DTPS) framework is proposed. Bandwidth and power budgets couple slow-timescale slice-level budgeting with fast-timescale MAC-level radio resource scheduling and drive selective, user-level-QoS-oriented allocation. At the slice level, an environment-dynamics prediction model (world model) learns the evolution of budget-conditioned QoS feasibility and characterizes how different bandwidth and power budgets affect future user-level QoS satisfaction. To cope with partial observability, a prior–posterior state-space predictor combined with prediction-based policy learning and online posterior correction generates foresighted budget decisions under incomplete observation and traffic bursts. At the MAC level, a QoS-Class-Identifier (QCI)–priority two-dimensional knapsack scheduler maps the given bandwidth and power budgets into a joint resource-block-group (RBG)–power allocation problem and reduces resource consumption through minimum RBG–power feasibility tests, maximizing the priority-weighted QoS satisfaction ratio under limited resources. In Starlink-scale simulations, DTPS attains an 80.8\% user-level QoS satisfaction ratio under overloaded traffic, well above the 33.0\%--56.3\% of representative baselines, keeps the satisfaction variation below 0.1 percentage points across burst intensities, and maintains a normalized resource consumption of 0.839 and an average delay of 78.3 ms.

(4) For semantic-reliable transmission under constraints, to address the prohibitive feedback and retransmission cost of semantic delivery under limited onboard computing, heterogeneous service inputs, and excessive full-retransmission overhead, a Coordinated Incremental Semantic Hybrid Automatic Repeat Request (CIS-HARQ) mechanism is proposed to achieve reliable end-to-end semantic delivery through lightweight closed-loop incremental transmission. Without deploying a large or task-specific semantic-evaluation model at the transmitter, the receiver generates a top-$K$ hard-mask feedback from the semantic distortion and returns it over a low-rate control channel, turning receiver-side distortion sensing into the basis for transmitter-side increment generation. Based on this feedback, a feedback-conditioned refinement encoder with an HARQ-round embedding produces selective semantic increments from past feedback and the current round, avoiding repeated transmission of the full semantic representation. At the receiver, a mask-guided semantic accumulation mechanism fuses and updates the multi-round increments according to the distortion mask, progressively improving the semantic representation under limited bandwidth and computing. By unifying feedback, incremental encoding, and semantic accumulation into a closed loop, the mechanism reduces retransmission overhead while enhancing end-to-end semantic reliability under heterogeneous inputs. On ImageNet-100 at SNR = 5 dB and tight bandwidth (CBR $\approx$ 0.02), CIS-HARQ improves the downstream task accuracy from 35.7\% for the equal-bandwidth baseline to 65.0\% over three refinement rounds, at far lower model size and latency than generative baselines.

In summary, targeting user-level end-to-end QoS provisioning in large-scale LEO constellations without reliable global state, with weak central coordination and limited onboard resources, this dissertation develops a distributed cooperative resource-scheduling methodology. Built around four key protocol-stack subproblems—network-state perception and distributed routing, access and mobility management, cross-layer radio resource scheduling, and semantic-reliable transmission under constraints—it constructs locally executable, learning-driven scheduling mechanisms that make routing states reliable, access fair, user-level QoS attainable, and semantic delivery reliable. Starlink-scale simulations show that the four mechanisms achieve significant gains in routing delay, access fairness, user-level QoS satisfaction under overloaded traffic, and task accuracy under tight bandwidth, providing a systematic solution for the transition of large-scale LEO constellations from layered isolated optimization toward QoS-oriented distributed cooperative control.
\end{abstract}
