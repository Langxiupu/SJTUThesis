\begin{abstract}[zh]
IMT-2030 6G 愿景中，低轨（Low Earth Orbit，LEO）卫星网络已从补充覆盖系统扩展为非地面接入、星间回传与全球业务承载的空间基础设施。再生式载荷与星间链路使 LEO 星座的网络管控从地面集中配置转向星上自治决策。然而，大规模 LEO 星座面临拓扑高速变化、业务时空分布非均匀、星间回传容量受限以及星上资源有限等约束。传统的全网状态汇聚与集中式调度难以直接应用。6G 空天地一体化网络需要 LEO 星座实现分布式资源管控和用户级服务质量（Quality of Service，QoS）保障。

用户级 QoS 保障需要网络状态估计、接入控制、资源调度与跨层传输协同工作，同时满足通信可靠性、资源分配效率和接入公平性。星上分布式管控面临四类关键问题：退化遥测破坏本地链路负载与拓扑视图并扩散低可信状态；多重覆盖与高动态可见关系使弱覆盖用户难以维持接入服务的持续可用性；切片层预算对未来用户级 QoS 的支撑能力难以预测；星上轻量化语义传输难以平衡编码器复杂度、推理时延和传输性能。本文提出大规模 LEO 星座的分布式协同资源管控方法，通过本地状态校准、有限邻域信令交互和跨节点协作替代全网同步与集中式求解。设计四项技术：多源置信度调控的分布式网络状态感知、基于局部公平性估计的分布式协作接入切换、基于生成式网络状态感知的切片--MAC 分层协作优化与基于任务反馈的轻量化语义传输。

（1）本文提出多源置信度调控的分布式网络状态感知框架（CA-LDP）。CA-LDP 将本地状态感知分解为状态校准与置信度约束传播两阶段。状态校准阶段联合利用译码观测、时序先验、邻域空间支撑和信息新鲜度对退化遥测进行置信度自适应图信号修复。传播阶段通过置信度加权聚合限制低可信状态的传播强度。在 1584 颗卫星的 Starlink 星座和九类受控遥测退化场景下，CA-LDP 相比基线方法将平均传输速率提升 $4.8\%$--$10.8\%$，端到端时延降低 $6.7\%$--$11.1\%$，路由成功率接近 $100\%$。

（2）针对多重覆盖与高动态可见关系使弱覆盖用户难以维持接入服务持续可用性的问题，本文提出基于局部公平性估计的 FG-MAPPO 方法。在线切换时，FG-MAPPO 将用户长期累计接入效用作为邻域信令，使终端低开销感知邻域长期服务状态。训练时采用广义 Gini 公平聚合函数构造公平性修正训练信号，通过吞吐优先--公平修正（TOFO）分立结构降低对效率与公平性这对冲突目标的值估计方差。该机制兼容 3GPP 条件切换（CHO）流程，仅使用本地观测和有限邻域信令联合考虑候选卫星的链路质量、负载状态和长期公平性。仿真表明，在仅交互邻域信息时，FG-MAPPO 的广义 Gini 公平性指标较集中式规划提升 $3.48\%$，平均速率提升 $6.91\%$。相比分布式强化学习基线，切换成功率与负载均衡性能最高分别提升 $15.65\%$ 与 $30.91\%$，邻域信令开销仅 $7.73$ kB/s。

（3）针对切片层预算对未来用户级 QoS 支撑能力难以预测的问题，本文提出基于生成式网络状态感知的 DTPS 框架。切片层通过生成式网络状态感知学习候选预算下未来 QoS 演化关系，为 MAC 层提供能实现 QoS 保障的长期资源预算。MAC 层估计候选流满足速率--时延门限的最小资源开销，筛除不可满足流并执行 QCI 优先级加权选择。Starlink 规模星座仿真中，DTPS 在过载业务下实现 $80.8\%$ 的用户级 QoS 满足率，而 TDRL-RSUA、RL-JRSS、LACO 与 DQN-MAC 等基线方法的 QoS 满足率为 $33.0\%$--$56.3\%$。DTPS 平均时延保持在 $78.3$ ms。当突发不确定性增强时，QoS 满足率维持在 $97.6\%$ 以上。

（4）针对星上轻量化语义传输难以平衡编码器复杂度、推理时延和传输性能的问题，本文提出基于任务反馈与协同增量重传的 CIS-HARQ 机制。该机制将可靠性增益从模型复杂度扩展转向通信轮次与反馈协作，将完整重传重构为任务反馈引导的选择性增量编码与跨轮合并。接收端根据当前语义状态与任务特征差距生成反馈，发射端依据反馈生成受码率约束的语义增量，接收端逐轮恢复任务相关特征。在 ImageNet-100、CIFAR-100 与 VeRi-776 数据集、Rician 衰落信道、信噪比 $5$ dB 且信道带宽比（CBR）约 $0.02$ 的紧带宽条件下，CIS-HARQ 经三轮增量传输将下游任务精度由同带宽基线的 $35.7\%$ 提升至 $65.0\%$。该机制参数量约 $2.2$ M，星上推理时延毫秒级，较生成式扩散接收端参数量减少约 $2500$ 倍，任务精度差距控制在约 $7\%$ 以内。

所提方法覆盖网络状态感知、接入切换、资源分配与语义传输的分布式协同管控。星座规模仿真表明，在全局状态缺失且地面控制受限时，可有效改善状态感知可靠性、接入公平性、过载下的 QoS 满足率及紧带宽下的任务精度。本文将大规模 LEO 星座资源管控从分层独立优化推进至端到端 QoS 导向的分布式协同管控，在有限邻域信令与可控星上算力约束下，为 6G 空天地一体化网络的星上自治提供技术路径。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract}[en]
Under the IMT-2030 6G vision, low Earth orbit (LEO) satellite networks have expanded from complementary coverage systems into space infrastructure for non-terrestrial access, inter-satellite backhaul, and globally continuous services. Regenerative payloads and inter-satellite links enable network management in LEO constellations to shift from ground-centric centralized configuration to onboard autonomous decision-making. However, large-scale LEO constellations face constraints such as rapidly varying topology, nonuniform spatiotemporal traffic distribution, constrained inter-satellite backhaul capacity, and limited onboard resources. Conventional global-state collection and centralized scheduling are difficult to apply directly. 6G space-air-ground integrated networks require LEO constellations to achieve distributed resource management and user-level quality-of-service (QoS) assurance.

User-level QoS assurance requires coordination among network-state estimation, access control, resource scheduling, and cross-layer transmission to satisfy communication reliability, resource-allocation efficiency, and access fairness simultaneously. Onboard distributed management faces four key challenges: degraded telemetry disrupts local link-load and topology views and propagates low-confidence states; multiple coverage and highly dynamic visibility relationships make it difficult for weak-coverage users to maintain continuous access-service availability; the capability of slice-layer budgets to support future user-level QoS is hard to predict; and onboard lightweight semantic transmission struggles to balance encoder complexity, inference latency, and transmission performance. This dissertation proposes a distributed cooperative resource-management method for large-scale LEO constellations, replacing global-state synchronization and centralized scheduling with local state calibration, limited neighborhood signaling exchange, and cross-node cooperation. Four techniques are designed: distributed network-state awareness with multi-source confidence regulation, distributed cooperative access-handover based on local fairness estimation, slice--MAC hierarchical cooperative optimization based on generative network-state awareness, and lightweight semantic transmission based on task feedback and coordinated incremental retransmission.

To address the issue that degraded telemetry disrupts local link-load and topology views and propagates low-confidence states, this dissertation proposes a distributed network-state awareness framework with multi-source confidence regulation (CA-LDP). CA-LDP decomposes local state awareness into two stages: state calibration and confidence-constrained propagation. The state-calibration stage jointly exploits decoded observations, temporal priors, neighborhood spatial support, and information freshness to recover degraded telemetry through confidence-adaptive graph-signal restoration. The propagation stage uses confidence-weighted aggregation to limit the propagation strength of low-confidence states. In a 1584-satellite Starlink constellation and nine controlled telemetry-degradation scenarios, CA-LDP improves the average transmission rate by $4.8\%$--$10.8\%$ and reduces end-to-end delay by $6.7\%$--$11.1\%$ compared to baseline methods, with routing success ratio close to $100\%$.

To address the issue that multiple coverage and highly dynamic visibility relationships make it difficult for weak-coverage users to maintain continuous access-service availability, this dissertation proposes the FG-MAPPO method based on local fairness estimation. During online handover, FG-MAPPO uses each user's long-term cumulative access utility as neighborhood signaling, enabling terminals to sense the long-term service state of the neighborhood with low overhead. During training, this method employs generalized Gini fairness aggregation to construct fairness-corrected training signals and uses the throughput-oriented fairness-correction (TOFO) decoupled structure to reduce value-estimation variance for the conflicting objectives of efficiency and fairness. The mechanism is compatible with the 3GPP Conditional Handover (CHO) process and jointly considers candidate-link quality, satellite load, and long-term fairness using only local observations and limited neighborhood signaling. Simulations show that with only neighborhood information exchange, FG-MAPPO improves the generalized Gini fairness metric by $3.48\%$ and the average rate by $6.91\%$ compared to a centralized planning reference. Compared with distributed reinforcement-learning baselines, it improves the handover success ratio and load-balancing performance by up to $15.65\%$ and $30.91\%$, respectively, with neighborhood signaling overhead of only $7.73$ kB/s.

To address the issue that the capability of slice-layer budgets to support future user-level QoS is hard to predict, this dissertation proposes the DTPS framework based on generative network-state awareness for slice--MAC hierarchical cooperative optimization. At the slice layer, based on generative network-state awareness, DTPS learns the evolution of future QoS under candidate budgets, thereby providing long-term resource budgets that enable QoS assurance for the MAC layer. At the MAC layer, DTPS estimates the minimum resource cost for each candidate flow to satisfy its rate--delay threshold, filters out unsatisfiable flows, and performs QCI-priority weighted selection. In Starlink-scale simulations, DTPS achieves a user-level QoS satisfaction ratio of $80.8\%$ under overloaded traffic, while baseline methods such as TDRL-RSUA, RL-JRSS, LACO, and DQN-MAC achieve QoS satisfaction ratios of $33.0\%$--$56.3\%$. DTPS keeps the average delay at $78.3$ ms. When burst uncertainty increases, the QoS satisfaction ratio remains above $97.6\%$.

To address the issue that onboard lightweight semantic transmission struggles to balance encoder complexity, inference latency, and transmission performance, this dissertation proposes the CIS-HARQ mechanism based on task feedback and coordinated incremental retransmission. This mechanism shifts reliability gain from model-complexity expansion to communication rounds and feedback cooperation, reformulating full retransmission as task-feedback-guided selective incremental encoding and cross-round combining. The receiver generates feedback from the gap between the current semantic state and task features, the transmitter generates rate-constrained semantic increments conditioned on this feedback, and the receiver progressively recovers task-relevant features. On the ImageNet-100, CIFAR-100, and VeRi-776 datasets over a Rician fading channel, at an SNR of $5$ dB and a tight channel bandwidth ratio (CBR) of about $0.02$, CIS-HARQ improves downstream task accuracy from $35.7\%$ for the equal-bandwidth baseline to $65.0\%$ after three incremental transmission rounds. With a parameter count of about $2.2$ M and millisecond-level onboard inference latency, the parameter count is reduced by about $2500$ times compared to a generative diffusion receiver, while the task-accuracy gap is kept within about $7\%$.

The proposed methods cover distributed cooperative management of network-state awareness, access handover, resource allocation, and semantic transmission. Constellation-scale simulations show that when global states are unavailable and ground control is limited, the methods effectively improve state awareness reliability, access fairness, QoS satisfaction under overload, and task accuracy under tight bandwidth. This dissertation advances resource management for large-scale LEO constellations from isolated layer-wise optimization to end-to-end QoS-oriented distributed cooperative management, and provides a technical path toward onboard autonomy in 6G space-air-ground integrated networks under the constraints of limited neighborhood signaling and controllable onboard computing budgets.
\end{abstract>
